## Preserve uncertainty during later analysis

A recommendation is not an implemented change; an implemented change is not a demonstrated cause of a result. An exact quotation establishes wording, not truth. Separate revenue, profit, cash, borrowed proceeds, and equity valuations. Resolve the speaker before assigning a belief to a channel owner. Titles and automatic guides can overstate what the reviewed evidence establishes.

There is an unresolved inconsistency between Nick's broad statement that he never sold a business and a separate detailed sale account. Both are retained: [the broad statement](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/uflyGsDqoYk.md) and [the sale account](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/RoTui9BCW4k.md). Do not silently reconcile them without additional evidence.

The [topic index](topics.md) supports exploration and the [structured insights](insights.jsonl) preserve source-level provenance. Shared tags do not establish agreement. Lack of a coded insight is not evidence that a creator never addresses a subject.

## Comparisons sharpened by complete readings

**Passive allocation versus active ownership.** [Sahil's financial habits](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/v5wpqSM0Ck0.md) recommend automating index investing while focusing effort on earning capacity. [Codie's private-market argument](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/NyVBWIW6vbk.md) emphasizes active ownership but explicitly concedes index funds are a reasonable default and requires acquisition diligence. This is not a clean argument over whether recurring investments work. Compare operating labor, leverage, liquidity, concentration, acquisition price, and net owner cash before comparing claimed returns.

**What is time being bought back for?** In [Dan's AI buyback loop](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/oB5zo5G7KRg.md), the stated destination is business growth. [Sahil's anti-goals](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/EvMEBzrDQVA.md) put explicit limits on sacrificing joy, health, and relationships. [Codie's routine](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/yBefMa8LydA.md) protects evenings and recovery as well as leverage work. These are source-specific objectives, not permanent or mutually exclusive creator identities.

**Public work as distribution and practice.** [Alex's documentation advice](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/yb2cLMMuMdQ.md) preserves beginner effort and lessons; [Nick's media account](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/PV3FS0M00T4.md) includes communication practice and company support; [Sahil's endorsement story](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/MvDvncmy2dA.md) includes an explicit ask and years of follow-through. A public audience can have several roles. None of these accounts isolates a repeatable causal return from publishing.

**Prepared context versus indiscriminate context.** [Dan's knowledge-system proposal](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/b4d32pBa3UY.md) emphasizes structured extracted records and source pointers. His [forty AI lessons](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/Gqoez9-Ij8w.md) warn that irrelevant context can obscure what matters and require named workflow owners. Together they suggest evaluating retrieval relevance, provenance, and maintenance, rather than using note count or a graph's appearance as evidence that a system understands the material.

**Agency alongside entry conditions.** [Nick's first paid work](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/acCBdQ423Do.md) includes family-provided customer access. [Alex's capacity list](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/EonibwnAEME.md) includes savings, practice, rest, and relationships. [Sahil's entrepreneurship discussion](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/55QLv6XIwrs.md) includes borrowing and benefits friction. Preserve those conditions when coding a message as self-reliance; do not make belief the sole explanatory variable.

For the corrected interpretations, limitations, and pending work, see [deeper analysis findings](deeper-analysis.md).

## Conditions that explain apparently similar advice

**Demand, delivery and scope come before a growth tactic.** [Alex's capacity test](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/OQf2Ba-Lp_4.md) asks whether twice the leads can be served. [Dan's manual-to-MVP sequence](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/K8Ros5RhJW4.md) asks whether the service is useful and can be repeated before software scales it. [Codie's local AI proposal](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/rMvTyvbLoy0.md) starts from a bottleneck and includes handoff. [Nick's intervention](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/El0u9vl0dy8.md) temporarily returns the founder to delivery to repair it. These can be stages of one cycle: prove value, stabilize delivery, document, delegate, then acquire more demand. This is an editorial organizing hypothesis, not a jointly stated doctrine.

**Brand and sales are connected through delivery.** [Alex's product-brand loop](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/8C_6qojTA78.md) says the product confirms or breaks the promise. [Dan's creator-business advice](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/oUdNG82hnyA.md) treats trust and operating capability as prerequisites to monetization. [Nick's media account](../../../creators/nick-huber/sources/PV3FS0M00T4.md) mixes communication practice, relationships and support of operating companies. The shared topic is credible distribution; the revenue mechanism differs.

**An employee's transition is not an owner's reinvestment decision.** [Codie](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/gF5ezwchWBs.md) discusses salary negotiation, saving and verification before acquisition; [Sahil](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/6tjldo4yXCQ.md) explicitly validates employment. [Alex's leadership-hiring case](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/0EqJD2o-Mnk.md) begins with an already profitable firm. Preserve starting capital, dependants, benefits, debt and operating skill when assessing whether advice transfers.

**Financial freedom has several denominators.** [Sahil's return-on-hassle framing](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/Fj8HsHqyMA0.md) includes attention; [Dan's buyback heuristic](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/7KLDM--MI6Q.md) prices delegable time; [Alex's capital matrix](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/sL16tsGafcQ.md) adds control and investor responsibility; [Codie's affluent-service ladder](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/olaz2NJeUUI.md) exposes the dependence inside high pay. Compare net cash, hours, control, liquidity and downside separately. None is a universal scalar measure of freedom.

## Disagreements and tensions to keep open

- **Public accountability versus secrecy:** Codie recommends [building publicly](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/a20aMsJ756I.md) and elsewhere [moving silently](../../../creators/codie-sanchez/sources/D2umgh70Kis.md). No source-backed rule resolves when each applies. Do not invent a private-build/public-launch reconciliation.
- **AI augmentation versus workforce removal:** Dan's [augmentation material](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/mydzICwDb6c.md) favors human capability, while the [zero-employee examples](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/b3yuAekDS4U.md) still need people and include substitution. Alex's [task-level workforce argument](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/9q5ojtkqsBs.md) explicitly accepts removing non-adapting staff. Sahil's [collective-incentives analysis](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/AHb_AFtC-Yk.md) asks who receives productivity gains. Operational capability, employer preference and social consequences are distinct questions.
- **Wellbeing versus the exceptional push:** Sahil's [nonpunitive habit rule](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/25ngHHTohRA.md) and [ordinary good-life choices](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/qONyYfpI-5Y.md) coexist with [launch pressure](../../../creators/sahil-bloom/sources/vvMaFyr_214.md) and some sleep-sacrificing networking advice. Dan's [growth-directed use of recovered time](../../../creators/dan-martell/sources/oB5zo5G7KRg.md) and broader life pillars also set competing objectives. Record the source's actual priority rather than assigning a permanently balanced or permanently growth-maximizing identity.

## Questions the available evidence can and cannot answer

The notes support questions about stated criteria, proposed workflows, contradictions, commercial context and reported examples. They do not support creator return rankings, causal estimates of coaching effectiveness, prevalence estimates for the entire channel or conclusions about X writing. In particular, [the railing case](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/HGZOxBfnF-E.md) supplies a reported outcome below its earlier forecast, whereas [the cleaning case](../../../creators/alex-hormozi/sources/OQf2Ba-Lp_4.md) supplies a revenue trajectory/goal and lead counts. They are different evidence types despite similar titles.

When coding the corpus later, retain the source's review scope, attributed speaker, business stage, intended outcome, proposed action, evidence type, commercial interest and unresolved limit. Absence of a coded idea is not evidence that the creator never discusses it. The [whole-video records](video-reviews.jsonl) preserve context that cannot fit into a single quote.
