# Present proposals live during calls to handle client objections

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Get Rich for Service-Based Businesses (Thank Me Later)

Alex emphasizes that sales teams must review invoices directly during live conversations rather than sending static emails. Presenting terms live allows representatives to address immediate concerns and resolve prospect hesitations.

> never present an invoice via email should always be over the phone because they're going to have questions and you want to be the person who can answer those questions
