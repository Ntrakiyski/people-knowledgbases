These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Get Rich for Service-Based Businesses (Thank Me Later)

Alex explains that entrepreneurs limit business potential by pursuing excessive initiatives simultaneously. Achieving substantial scale requires selecting high-return acquisition channels and ruthlessly removing secondary activities that dilute team focus.

> a lot of things can work but none of them will work if you try to do them all
