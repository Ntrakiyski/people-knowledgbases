These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In a video to watch if you're ambitious and in your 20s or 30s

The speaker contends that individuals often remain in familiar activities simply because they understand how to win, avoiding the discomfort of starting as a beginner in higher-leverage fields like business management and marketing.

> just because you know how to win doesn't mean you should win and it doesn't mean the game's worth playing
