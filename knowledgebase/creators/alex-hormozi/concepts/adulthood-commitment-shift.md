These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In It took me 37 years to realize what I'll tell you in 18 minutes.

Alex contrasts youthful optionality with adult commitment, noting that open choices carry zero intrinsic value until executed. Achieving substantial life progress requires closing secondary doors to dedicate effort toward chosen priorities.

> the goal of youth is optionality and the goal of adulthood is commitment and these two things are at odds
