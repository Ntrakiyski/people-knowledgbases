## In How The Top 1% Actually Think About Trust

Keeping a promise and giving useful advice build a different kind of confidence from not exploiting secrets. The transcript distinguishes these mechanisms rather than treating trust as one undifferentiated trait.

> If you say "I'm going to pick up the kid," and then you do the likelihood that they'll trust you pick up the kid in the future goes up
