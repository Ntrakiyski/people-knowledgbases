# Executive decision making provides higher leverage than automating low priority work

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Why AI won't make you rich in 2026

The speaker asserts that determining which non-essential projects to eliminate provides greater leverage than automating lower-priority activities. Focusing limited team resources on primary constraints generates superior operational efficiency.

> it is more efficient for me to determine that something is not a priority than for me to automate something that is not a priority
