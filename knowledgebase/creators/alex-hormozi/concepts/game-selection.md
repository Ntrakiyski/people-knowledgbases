# Uncritical competition leads to unrewarding achievements

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In a video to watch if you're ambitious and in your 20s or 30s

The speaker warns against dedicating effort to competitive pursuits without evaluating whether the ultimate prize justifies the required sacrifices. Competing in ill-suited domains often results in winning outcomes that lack meaningful personal or professional value.

> if you play stupid games you win stupid prizes i'll say that again play dumb games win dumb prizes
