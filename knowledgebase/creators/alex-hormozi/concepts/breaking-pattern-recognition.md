# Break predictable audience patterns to stand out

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In If I Started A Personal Brand In 2026, I'd Do This - Dolly Parton

A participant explains that audiences rely on pattern recognition to make quick assumptions about people based on superficial cues. Conforming to standard tropes makes an individual predictable and obscure. Intentionally breaking expected patterns captures viewer attention by defying standard social categorization.

> you have to break the pattern You have to be what they don't expect or they don't predict
