## In If I could start over in business, I’d do this sooner

The speaker argues that forcing top talent to collaborate with underperforming peers creates frustration and demotivation. Maintaining high talent standards preserves morale and helps organizations attract and retain capable team members.

> there's nothing more demotivating to a winner than being forced to work with a loser
