## In Can I Save This Failing Theme Park in 90 Days?

An advisor recommends placing trust indicators, customer ratings, and safety assurances directly adjacent to primary call-to-action buttons on web pages. Structuring landing pages to address parent safety concerns reduces friction and improves lead capture efficiency.

> you want to put trust right next to the action so you have this parent rating right around the button
