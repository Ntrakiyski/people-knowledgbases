## Context and limits

Preserve temporal language: 'as of today' the advisory practice is the identified higher-value service, whereas the network is something they 'will probably have.' A date mentioned in the speech does not establish launch. Portfolio revenue, revenue earned by the parent, distributions, cash available for reinvestment, and equity value are different quantities. Insurance, lending, and sales-AI examples are opportunities, not verified current operations. 'School' in the export refers to Skool in context.
