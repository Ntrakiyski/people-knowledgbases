The speaker contends that artificial intelligence introduces operational leverage without invalidating existing forms such as capital, media reach, and human teams. Business owners who adopt automated software without identifying core constraints often execute low-priority tasks faster rather than increasing overall profitability. Establishing clear strategic focus and determining which projects to eliminate yields higher operational leverage than automating non-essential activities.
