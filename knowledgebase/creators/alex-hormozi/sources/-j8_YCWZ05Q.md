## Context and limits

Platform counts, benchmark churn, cohort changes and reported case outcomes are not independently verified experiments. Lower later-tenure churn may reflect selection, not the causal effect of merely delaying cancellation. Simple inverse-churn LTV assumes stable rates/price and differs from profit value. Joins versus cancels predicts MRR only with suitable revenue assumptions. Annual billing changes observation and selection as well as behavior; he acknowledges several such mechanisms.
