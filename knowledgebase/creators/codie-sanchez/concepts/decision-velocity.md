## In 20 Years Of Money Advice in 22 Minutes (How I Made Millions)

Codie Sanchez contends that making rapid choices allows businesses to gather feedback and course-correct quickly. Prolonged indecision carries higher hidden costs than making reversible mistakes.

> a fast wrong answer is almost always cheaper than a slow right one
