# Exceptionally high proposal close rates signal underpriced services

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In I Built A Million Dollar Business For A Subscriber in 23 Minutes

Codie Sanchez notes that experiencing an unusually high proposal acceptance rate indicates that service prices are set too low. Increasing prices until a portion of prospective buyers decline optimizes profit margins.

> i believe if you're closing at an 83% rate you're too cheap They're not saying no to you enough
