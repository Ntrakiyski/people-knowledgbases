# Internal Testing Should Precede External Commercialization

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In I Tried The “Easiest” Ways To Make Money With AI

The speaker contends that business owners should validate automated tools within their own operational workflows prior to offering them as commercial services to external clients. Internal deployment allows teams to streamline routine tasks even if public client acquisition proves challenging.

> if you're going to use a bot probably try it internally first use it yourself before you sell it to somebody else
