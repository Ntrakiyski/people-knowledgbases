These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Unfortunately, Sales Really Is This Simple (4 Steps)

Codie Sanchez asserts that successful sales strategies focus on identifying customer profiles already experiencing urgent problems. Matching solutions to predisposed buyers eliminates the friction of forced persuasion.

> you don't sell anyone anything you find the people already predisposed to want the thing that you are selling
