# Selling requires absolute conviction in product value

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Unfortunately, Sales Really Is This Simple (4 Steps)

Codie Sanchez contends that sales representatives must fully believe in their product's efficacy and pricing. Lacking conviction causes sellers to project personal financial doubts onto prospective clients.

> if you do not believe in what you sell quit get out of the company
