## In I Built A Million Dollar Business For A Subscriber in 23 Minutes

Codie Sanchez recommends evaluating how much target clients spend within a service category and designing offerings to capture a larger share of that budget. Expanding services to address related client needs increases overall revenue.

> Analyze how much your customer spends then increase the percentage you are of that customer spend That's how you make more money
