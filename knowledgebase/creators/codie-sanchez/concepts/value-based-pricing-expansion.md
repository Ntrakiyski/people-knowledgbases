## In Success is Hard Until You Understand This Formula

A speaker advises determining prices by assessing the tangible value delivered to clients rather than copying market standards. Value-based pricing captures higher margins while avoiding arbitrary rate limits.

> the number should follow the disaster it prevented or it should follow the opportunity it created not the minutes it took to prevent it
