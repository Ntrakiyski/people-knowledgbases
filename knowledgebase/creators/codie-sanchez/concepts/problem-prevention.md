These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In If You Don't Understand Leadership, You'll Never Win

Codie Sanchez cautions leaders against constantly solving operational fires for team members. Stepping in repeatedly creates dependence and prevents staff from developing essential problem-solving capabilities.

> every fire you personally extinguish is a fire your people never learn to prevent
