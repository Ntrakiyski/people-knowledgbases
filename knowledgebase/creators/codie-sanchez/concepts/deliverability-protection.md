These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In I Tried The “Easiest” Ways To Make Money With AI

As described in the video, high bounce rates in email campaigns risk damaging domain sender reputation, which can prevent critical operational messages from reaching inbox destinations. The speaker notes that verifying recipient addresses prior to dispatch mitigates domain flagging.

> bounces sound harmless but every email that bounces tells gmail and outlook your domain might be spam and if that happens even your good emails stop landing
