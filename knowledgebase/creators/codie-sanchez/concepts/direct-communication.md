These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Psychology of Sales (How We Sell $100M)

Codie Sanchez recommends bypassing forced small talk and directly addressing client objectives during sales conversations. High-value buyers appreciate straightforward communication that respects their schedule.

> people with money don't like having their time wasted and they also don't typically like small talk
