# $100M CEO Explains: 7 Habits That Hold Entrepreneurs Back

Entrepreneurs often hinder business growth by succumbing to routine operational distractions and unexamined work habits. Constant micro-interruptions fragment attention, requiring structured time blocking to separate administrative management from deep strategic work. Leaders also weaken team culture by failing to recognize individual contributions and neglecting to communicate a compelling mission. Furthermore, attempting to invent unique business playbooks from scratch increases failure risk compared to iterating on proven operational models. Finally, avoiding ruthless deal negotiation and retaining low-margin, demanding customers erodes enterprise profitability, necessitating periodic customer portfolio audits.
