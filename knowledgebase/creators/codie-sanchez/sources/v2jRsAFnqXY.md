Scaling a specialized service enterprise requires focusing operational resources on ideal client personas while explicitly declining low-margin prospects. Defining clear client boundaries allows service providers to tailor marketing messages without diluting brand positioning. Overcoming growth bottlenecks involves shifting to category wallet share pricing, raising fees until a portion of prospective buyers decline.
