The speaker outlines six strategic levers to accelerate business growth without relying exclusively on digital marketing. Business owners can expand margins by raising prices to reflect unique value and filtering out price-sensitive customers. Growth can also be achieved by adding new distribution channels, auditing expenses to eliminate non-revenue-generating costs, and acquiring cash-flowing businesses using seller financing. Furthermore, companies can increase revenue by offering complementary products to existing customers and hiring experienced talent who bring established client relationships.
