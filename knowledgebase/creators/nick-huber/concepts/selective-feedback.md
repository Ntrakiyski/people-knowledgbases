# Keep useful contradictory advice while rejecting ridicule

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In I Got Mocked for Starting This Business

The participant explicitly rejects an interpretation that entrepreneurs should never listen to criticism. Contrary opinions are described as having prevented mistakes. The distinction is between evaluating substantive feedback and letting social embarrassment determine the decision.

> I think I've also saved myself a lot of headache by listening to people who had opinions that contradicted my opinions
