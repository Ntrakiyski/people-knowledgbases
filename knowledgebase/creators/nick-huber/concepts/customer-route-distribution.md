## In I Built a $2.2M Business With Sidewalk Chalk

The chalk campaign was designed around where students lived and walked to class. The transferable principle is knowledge of audience distribution, rather than a claim that sidewalk advertising works in every market.

> I knew where my clients walked I knew where they lived I knew where they went to class
