# Audience relevance can matter before audience size

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Truth About Personal Branding

Huber argues that a modest audience can help a company if the people reached belong to its commercial niche. This is a targeting principle; no conversion rate or causal estimate is established.

> you can do that with a thousand followers if they're on LinkedIn or if they're in your niche
