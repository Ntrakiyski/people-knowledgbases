These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Hiring Secret That Changed My Business

The participant says the recruiting firm uses typing assessments and associates speed with fewer guarantee claims. This is a reported employer observation. Applying such a proxy elsewhere would require checking relevance to the job and validating whether it predicts the intended outcome.

> the faster they type the less likely they are for one of our clients to come back for a client guarantee
