These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Mindset That Made Me Millions

The speaker describes having sufficient momentum and hedging to prevent one mistake from sinking him. His present resilience is a condition of the story, rather than a promise that early entrepreneurial bets are safe.

> there's not a single decision that has the opportunity to sink me
