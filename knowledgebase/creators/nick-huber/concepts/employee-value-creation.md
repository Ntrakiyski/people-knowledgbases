These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Why Most People Never Start

The intended application explicitly includes adding value inside a company and developing a well-paid career. The clip does not restrict agency, selling, or judgment to people who start firms.

> it's very very practical for just being a high performing individual adding value to a company getting paid well for it
