A participant describes first using an international recruiting firm as a customer and later becoming an investor and buyer. The conversation distinguishes starting a business from acquiring one, arguing that starting can develop the operating skills a buyer needs. Recruiting advice emphasizes testing relevant work before hiring and actively approaching capable people who may already be employed. The participant also reports using typing assessments as a screening proxy.
