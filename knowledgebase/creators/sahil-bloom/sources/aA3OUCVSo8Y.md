The pursuit map places specific work and nonwork activities on competence and energy axes. High competence with low energy is labeled a danger zone because rewards can prolong an unsatisfying commitment. High energy with high competence is the desired zone; high energy with low competence is potential worth developing. Bloom then proposes four actions: adjust a draining activity, cut one or work toward cutting it, protect recurring time for a strong energizing pursuit, and add exploratory interests. The framework is a self-assessment tool rather than a validated career diagnosis.
