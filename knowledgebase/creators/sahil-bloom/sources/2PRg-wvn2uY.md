Bloom distinguishes management, creation, consumption and ideation. Batch necessary coordination into discrete windows; protect creation when energy is high; deliberately consume varied inputs; and leave stimulus-free space to connect ideas. His exercise is a week of calendar color-coding followed by rebuilding one more balanced day. He acknowledges less calendar control early in a career.
