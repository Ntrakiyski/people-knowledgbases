Bloom adapts the story of Bill Gates's think week into a shorter reflection session with a journal in a fresh, secluded setting. Five questions examine repeated daily habits, what an observer would infer about priorities, obvious action seen from outside, important versus merely urgent work, and behaviors a future self may regret. The suggested cadence is monthly or quarterly, with a session lasting a few hours. The purpose is to create perspective before changing action. Billionaire associations and promised asymmetric outcomes are motivational framing, not evidence that the exercise reproduces another person's success.
