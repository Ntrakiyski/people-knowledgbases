These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to quickly get out of a rut

Bloom explicitly discourages compensatory extra work. The preferred reset is returning to the routine.

> if you miss a day don't try to make up for it and do extra the next day
