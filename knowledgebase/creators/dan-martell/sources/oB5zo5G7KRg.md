Martell adapts his buyback loop to AI: provide relevant context, audit the calendar against goals, transfer repeatable tasks, and fill the space with activities that both create income and supply energy. Suggested workflows include calendar review, shorter or fewer meetings, recorded task demonstrations, email-pattern analysis, and AI drafting. He says to try AI before adding a full-time hire, with documented instructions available for a human if needed. Crucially, he explicitly directs recovered hours toward building the business rather than a four-hour workweek.
