Martell describes a personal knowledge system, favoring Obsidian Markdown while naming Drive and Notion as alternatives. Three files describe the user, assistant behavior, and assistant role. Seven folders organize people, projects, decisions, companies, meetings, daily notes, and reusable knowledge; optional maps of content link related material. Meeting extraction captures decisions, commitments, preferences, and insights while retaining pointers to original material. A nightly process is meant to create missing entity notes, merge duplicates, update links, and flag strategic items. The video promotes a company operating-system playbook.
