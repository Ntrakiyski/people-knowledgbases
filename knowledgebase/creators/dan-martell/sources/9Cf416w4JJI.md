Unblocking enterprise growth requires identifying and resolving the single primary bottleneck constraining workflow throughput. The speaker presents a four-step framework: mapping business processes with conversion metrics, surging team resources onto the identified constraint, running controlled single-variable experiments, and empowering employees with financial decision authority. By testing hypotheses against statistical data and establishing clear metric ownership, business leaders eliminate operational drag and expand organizational capacity predictably.
