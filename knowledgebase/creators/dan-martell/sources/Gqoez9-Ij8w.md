# 40 Hacks to Use AI So Well It Feels Unfair

This page describes the selected source's claims and advice. The summary and interpretations are machine-extracted drafts; quotations have been matched against the exported transcript.

Review scope: Codex editorial draft; complete local transcript read; claims not independently verified.

Method: Direct Codex extraction from complete local transcript; exact quotations verified locally.

Martell's forty lessons move from prompting to operating design. He favors relevant context, examples, explicit instructions, iterative review, skepticism about accuracy, and business metrics. Later sections emphasize documenting stable work, assigning a named owner, finishing a useful workflow, and retaining human conversations while automating repetitive tasks. He also advocates AI-native hiring and leadership adoption. The source contains unresolved tensions: stop chasing models yet test many models, constrain context yet document everything, and work less while aiming for larger business outcomes. Claims that every describable task can be automated or that technical limits are only mindset exceed the evidence presented.
