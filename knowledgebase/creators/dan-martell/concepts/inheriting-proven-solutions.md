These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Make Rich Friends

The speaker recommends finding people who have already worked through a relevant challenge and learning from their experience. Relationships may provide useful context and introductions. The advice still needs to fit the learner’s circumstances; the conversation does not independently verify the time savings claimed.

> Every problem you have somebody else out there has already solved it If you build that relationship with them then you get to inherit the solution
