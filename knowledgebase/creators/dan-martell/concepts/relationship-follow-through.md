# Close the loop after receiving advice

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Make Rich Friends

The speaker recommends acting on specific advice and telling the person what happened. Reporting back shows that their response was used and gives a developing relationship a concrete continuation rather than leaving the effort with the recipient.

> when they reply immediately thank them and tell them that you did something with it so that they know that time that reply was well used
