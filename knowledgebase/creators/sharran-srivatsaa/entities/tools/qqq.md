# Invesco QQQ Trust (QQQ)

> one of my favorite ETFs to invest in is called the Q's QQQ.

[This Money Mistake Is Costing You Millions in 2026 (Avoid THIS)](../../sources/this-money-mistake-is-costing-you-millions-in-2026-avoid-this-6TpYYgMCc3E.md), 0:19:36.

QQQ is Sharran’s example of a familiar technology-oriented equity holding whose costs he later examined more closely. He contrasts it with QQQM to make the long-term effect of investment fees concrete.

The lesson is to inspect expenses and the exposure being purchased rather than assume that familiar funds are cost equivalent. The video’s stated fee difference and descriptions of structure are attributed to Sharran. They are not a current comparison of fund documents. The example supports the fee-drag concept without establishing that switching funds is appropriate after accounting for a particular investor’s taxes, trading needs, and account circumstances.
