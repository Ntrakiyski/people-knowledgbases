# Vanguard Real Estate ETF (VNQ)

> ETF called VNQ

[Once I Understood This About Money, My Life Changed](../../sources/once-i-understood-this-about-money-my-life-changed-XoWE1QPMFek.md), 0:15:33.

VNQ is Sharran’s example of accessing real-estate ownership through a public fund before having the capital or desire to buy an entire property. It appears in both his tiny income-generating asset discussion and his comparison of property and business ownership.

The recurring principle is to begin with an accessible form of ownership, observe its income and behavior, and use that experience to learn. This is different from promising that every small holding will grow into a large portfolio. Sharran’s geographical descriptions and comparisons are approximate. The videos do not replace the fund’s holdings or risk disclosures, and his ownership example is not a tailored allocation for another person.
