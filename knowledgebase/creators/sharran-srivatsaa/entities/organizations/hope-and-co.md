# Hope & Co.

> Hopeen Co. from Amsterdam

[The Louisiana Purchase Tactic to Buy a Business for $0](../../sources/the-louisiana-purchase-tactic-to-buy-a-business-for-0-Bd7nAa1jckw.md), 0:12:52.

Hope & Co. appears alongside Baring Brothers in Sharran’s reconstruction of the Louisiana Purchase financing. He describes intermediaries supplying immediate liquidity against American obligations, allowing France to receive money before those obligations had been repaid.

The example separates the timing needs of the seller from the financing capacity of the buyer. A third party can make the exchange workable by accepting repayment risk and earning a spread. Historical figures and institutional roles are taken from the video’s narration. The case is used to explain financing mechanics, rather than document the original transaction or establish a precedent for contemporary borrowing.
