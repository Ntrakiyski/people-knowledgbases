# Game Freak

> Game Freak creates the game, Creatures Inc. supports and shares in the IP

[Pokémon’s $140B Deal Will Make You Rich (Here’s How)](../../sources/pok-mon-s-140b-deal-will-make-you-rich-here-s-how-YHigza2n5wU.md), 0:08:42.

Game Freak is the operating and development company in Sharran’s Pokémon case study. He describes a long development period, financial strain, and the need for outside backing. Earlier shipped work serves as evidence of capability, while continued development illustrates sweat equity.

The case connects three ideas: contributors can earn ownership through work, proof reduces the perceived risk of supporting them, and financing choices redistribute future rights. Sharran’s story should not be read as evidence that persistence alone guarantees commercial success. Its specific history and economics are presented through his narration; the durable lesson is how creative labor, funding, and distribution can hold different positions in the same venture.
