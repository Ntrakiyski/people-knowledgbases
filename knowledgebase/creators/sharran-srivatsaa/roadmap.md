# Coverage roadmap

See [status.md](status.md) for measured coverage and individual pending videos.

The initial catalog has been imported and synthesized. For future updates:

1. Refresh the catalog and obtain timestamped transcripts for new uploads.
2. Read each new transcript and extract substantive concepts, entities, and exact quotes.
3. Review aliases and extend the canonical taxonomy where the evidence warrants it.
4. Update affected synthesis bodies and regenerate summaries, pages, and indexes.
5. Validate structure and citations, then review the changed synthesis against its transcripts.

Refresh the catalog and repeat this process when new uploads are needed. No
recurring job has been configured.
