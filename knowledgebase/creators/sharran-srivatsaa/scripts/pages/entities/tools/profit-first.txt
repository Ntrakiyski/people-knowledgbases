Profit First is the allocation method Sharran uses as a starting point for his invest-first argument. He describes it as reversing the usual order of income minus expenses so that profit receives an explicit claim before spending absorbs what is available.

His extension is to ask what current profit does for the future owner. If all of it becomes lifestyle spending, it does not necessarily create assets or recurring income. The book’s role in the corpus is this substantive comparison between prioritizing present profit and systematically funding future investment. The video presents Sharran’s interpretation of the method rather than a complete summary of the book.
