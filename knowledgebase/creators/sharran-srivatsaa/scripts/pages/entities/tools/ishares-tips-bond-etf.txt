TIP appears both as an example of accessing inflation-protected Treasury exposure through a fund and as a component of Sharran’s four-fund income portfolio. He describes owning it and connects it to protecting purchasing power.

The videos simplify the relationship between the underlying securities and the fund. They do not comprehensively explain market pricing, real interest rates, taxes, or the effect of selling before a particular horizon. An account of CPI-linked securities therefore does not establish that the ETF’s sale price or total return is protected. The fund’s role here is the speaker’s implementation example, with those limits preserved.
