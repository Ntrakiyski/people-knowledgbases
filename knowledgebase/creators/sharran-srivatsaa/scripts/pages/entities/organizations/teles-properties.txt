Teles Properties is Sharran’s recurring example of moving from advising business owners to operating a company. He describes starting with a small brokerage, contributing money and work, learning alongside an investor, and eventually expanding and selling the business. Those growth and exit figures are his account.

The operating lesson centers on customer discovery. In his telling, agents valued gaining time more than the benefits management initially emphasized. The promise of saving an agent a day each week became a way to choose services and communicate value. Across the videos, the case therefore connects ownership, sweat equity, and a concise value proposition. The transcripts use both Teles and Telus; this page unifies the references while preserving exact ASR wording in quotations.
