Dave Matthews supplies Sharran’s example of questioning an assumed sequence. Sharran recounts a backstage conversation in which the musician described developing melody before fitting lyrics. An order Sharran had taken for granted turned out to be only one possible approach.

The lesson is to name the rule shaping a problem before optimizing within it. Similar assumptions may concern credentials, money, permission, or the order of work. Matthews’s role here is the subject of that anecdote; the source does not provide a comprehensive account of his creative process or establish that reversing sequence is always beneficial.
