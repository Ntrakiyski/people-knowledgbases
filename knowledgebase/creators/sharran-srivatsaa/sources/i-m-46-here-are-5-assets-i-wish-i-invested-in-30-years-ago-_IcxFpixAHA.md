# I’m 46. Here Are 5 Assets I Wish I Invested In 30 Years Ago:

Sharran broadens the idea of an asset beyond money to five resources: money, relationships, time, skill, and brand. He describes money as a problem-solving tool and learning as a potentially asymmetric use of it, because a capability can remain useful after the initial purchase. His personal examples include an unlimited learning budget for children and targeted coaches for specific skill gaps; the claimed financial returns are his interpretation.

For relationships he repeats his 10–10–forever rule of deeply supporting a small group without keeping a short-term scorecard. For time he recounts doing real-estate work in exchange for an ownership share. He then reframes income goals as questions about missing marketable skills, and brand as trusted association that can later support other ventures. The practical exercise is to ask which resource or combination can solve a problem, rather than assuming an immediate cash payment is the only possible exchange.

## Concepts covered

- [Intentional Resource Allocation](../concepts/intentional-resource-allocation.md): Investing means deliberately deploying available resources toward a chosen outcome.
- [Personal Brand and Trust](../concepts/personal-brand.md): Repeated behavior and useful proof create associations that can support future opportunities.
- [Relationship Capital](../concepts/relationship-capital.md): Build durable support through attention, useful contributions, and trust established before it is needed.
- [Skill Development](../concepts/skill-development.md): Acquire and combine useful capabilities through focused learning, practice, and real application.
- [Sweat equity](../concepts/sweat-equity.md): Ownership can be earned by contributing work and capability rather than only cash.
