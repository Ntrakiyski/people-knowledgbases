# How to Actually Buy a House in 2026 (My Exact System)

Sharran presents a twelve-step home-buying process grouped into four stages. Before searching, he recommends articulating the life event motivating the move, separating non-negotiables from preferences, choosing between a better house and a better location, clarifying deadlines, and deciding between cash and mortgage financing. He proposes interviewing agents about their process, current buyer mistakes, and compensation, then obtaining mortgage pre-approval while keeping the financial picture stable.

During the search, he urges buyers to consider conditionally available off-market homes and understand months of inventory and the seller’s pricing strategy. After an accepted offer, he describes inspection, financing, and appraisal contingencies as opportunities to investigate, renegotiate, or exit within the applicable periods. Closing then involves moving logistics, a final walkthrough, document signing, and funds transfer. These are his process recommendations and descriptions of transaction practice rather than a statement of universal contractual rights.
