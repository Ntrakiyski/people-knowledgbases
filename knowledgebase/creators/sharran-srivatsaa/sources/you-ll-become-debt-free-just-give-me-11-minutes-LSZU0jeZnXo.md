# You'll Become Debt Free, Just Give Me 11 Minutes

Srivatsaa separates consumer debt from borrowing used to acquire income producing assets, then compares three repayment methods. The debt snowball targets the smallest balance to create motivation and reduce the number of obligations. The debt avalanche targets the highest interest rate to reduce interest expense. He explains that accumulating interest can increase the balance on which future interest is charged, although his numerical example is illustrative rather than a realistic quoted card rate.

His preferred third option is exploring debt consolidation: replace several obligations with one balance and payment schedule. He argues that obtaining a proposal can reveal the whole debt picture, expose available rates, and simplify repayment. He does not demonstrate that consolidation always reduces total borrowing costs, and explicitly recommends reviewing fine print with an advisor, AI, or careful document reading. He closes by encouraging a specific plan and consistent payments, then redirecting that payment habit toward investing once balances reach zero. His national debt statistics and optimistic claims about consolidation are speaker claims, not independently established by the transcript.
