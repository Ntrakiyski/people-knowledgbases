# Hit These Money Milestones, You'll Become Financially Free

Sharran replaces status-driven financial targets with five operating milestones. First, active income should exceed monthly expenses; he illustrates increasing earnings through a marketable certification. Second, money should enter a quarterback account and be routed into separate lifestyle, tax, and surplus destinations. Third, asset location considers whether money sits in taxable or tax-advantaged accounts and, more broadly in his terminology, the tax consequences of residence. His simplified tax examples and security claims are presented as his approach rather than guarantees.

The fourth milestone gives cash a job through deliberate liquidity and return choices. He discusses high-yield savings, staggered CD maturities, and his personal mix of SCHO, SCHD, TIP, and SGOV. Those instruments have different risks; his quarter allocations and projected yields are personal examples. Only after these foundations does he discuss investing for ownership, naming broad-company and real-estate funds as accessible starting points. His TIGA approach emphasizes small income-generating positions and learning before large commitments, with VOO and VNQ used as family examples.

## Concepts covered

- [Automated Money Routing](../concepts/automated-money-routing.md): Distribute incoming money explicitly among lifestyle spending, taxes, and future assets.
- [Cash Management](../concepts/cash-management.md): Match liquid reserves and surplus holdings to access needs, returns, and the risks of each instrument.
- [Income Margin](../concepts/income-margin.md): The surplus between earnings and spending that funds future assets and financial resilience.
- [Tax Efficiency](../concepts/tax-efficiency.md): Assess how account placement, timing, and applicable rules affect after-tax investment outcomes.
- [Tiny Income Generating Assets](../concepts/tiny-income-generating-assets.md): Small productive assets offer an initial practical step toward recurring investment income.
