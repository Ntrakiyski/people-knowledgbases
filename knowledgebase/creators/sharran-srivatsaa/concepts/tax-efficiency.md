# Tax Efficiency

> Asset location is where your assets are located.

[After 31 Years of Investing, THIS Is My Exact System (Copy Me)](../sources/after-31-years-of-investing-this-is-my-exact-system-copy-me-e7jNOBjblwk.md), 0:12:40.

Sharran evaluates investments by what remains after taxes and fees, rather than by the most attractive advertised return. His tax discussion has two distinct questions: what activity creates the return, and where that activity occurs. Buying the same holding inside different accounts can therefore produce different outcomes.

He calls the second question asset location. It includes the legal account containing the investment and, more broadly in his terminology, the jurisdiction where the investor lives. His examples contrast taxable brokerage accounts with retirement accounts and charitable structures. He suggests locating frequent trading or covered-call activity in a tax-advantaged account while holding lower-turnover investments elsewhere. These are his proposed arrangements, not complete eligibility or implementation instructions.

Tax timing matters because money paid out cannot simultaneously remain invested. His real-estate exchange example emphasizes deferring a gain so more proceeds can enter the next property. Retirement contributions, employer matches, depreciation, and holding periods provide other illustrations. Deferral and permanent exemption are different claims; the videos' occasional language about no taxes should not erase later withdrawals or other conditions.

He also distinguishes preparation from planning. Filing a compliant return records what happened, while forward planning considers available lawful choices before a transaction. He recommends having a strategist explain and document proposed treatment with the preparer, rather than expecting the client to argue a half-understood idea. This is a distinction between services, not evidence that accountants generally cannot plan.

His claims about tax-code percentages, rates, state advantages, Bitcoin loss harvesting, and specific investment incentives remain simplified statements from the speaker. His own choice to remain in California also shows that the lowest-tax option is not his sole objective. The practical framework makes tax consequences explicit while retaining the person's wider goals and constraints.
