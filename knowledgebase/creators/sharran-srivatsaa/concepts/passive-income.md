# Passive Income

> Passive income is prefunded income.

[Once I Hit These Money Milestones, I Became Financially Free](../sources/once-i-hit-these-money-milestones-i-became-financially-free-aetr9LovwE4.md), 0:13:25.

Sharran repeatedly defines passive income through what funds it: money or effort supplied before the later payments arrive. The common feature is a weaker connection between each payment and the recipient's current hours, rather than an absence of work anywhere in the system.

Capital-funded examples include dividend stocks, bonds, and rental-property interests. A person contributes money to an income-producing asset and receives distributions under that investment's terms. Effort-funded examples include educational products, royalties, referral links, and revenue-sharing arrangements. The initial contribution may be creating a product, building an audience, or establishing a useful commercial relationship.

His affiliate example makes the second route concrete. A recommendation carries a referral link; a purchase through that link can compensate the recommender. The video does not provide conversion rates, acquisition costs, or a calculation of the continuing work involved. It shows a payment mechanism, not proof that any recommendation will become a durable income stream.

The recipient's role is decisive. Personally managing rentals or maintaining a sales organization can still require active effort. Sharran explicitly says that something passive for one participant should be active work for someone else. His network-marketing example also includes maintenance despite its passive label.

Within his financial framework, these payments initially run alongside wages. Active work continues to fund living expenses while surplus builds assets and recurring income. Keeping lifestyle spending stable allows the new income to close the gap rather than merely finance a larger lifestyle.

The endpoint is expense coverage: passive income exceeding monthly costs makes work more optional in his definition. That relationship is more informative than a product being advertised as passive or an account showing an impressive balance.
