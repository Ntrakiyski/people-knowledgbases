# Tiny Income Generating Assets

> a tiny income generating asset, a Tiga.

[Passive Income Is Simple, Once You Have a Plan Like THIS:](../sources/passive-income-is-simple-once-you-have-a-plan-like-this-qi4gqXD4t-g.md), 0:05:52.

Srivatsaa uses the term TIGA for a small income producing holding that makes the first ownership step practical. The aim is to begin acquiring assets before someone can afford an entire business or rental property. A modest initial position provides both potential income and a concrete setting in which to learn how investing works.

## Start with the investing bunny hill

In his wealth teaching, a TIGA connects surplus earnings to the asset ownership rung of the ladder. A viewer can begin with a regular modest amount or an existing lump sum, observe how payments arrive, and establish the infrastructure for future investing. Srivatsaa argues that waiting until a large windfall arrives leaves the investor trying to build both knowledge and systems at the moment the stakes become larger.

His examples include small positions in business and real estate funds. He describes his son buying VOO and identifies VNQ as another route to ownership exposure without purchasing a building directly. Elsewhere, he offers an equally divided four fund portfolio, affiliate commissions, and pooling capital with friends to buy real estate. An Arnold Schwarzenegger property anecdote illustrates starting with a small asset, but its historical details remain the speaker’s account.

## Small is a starting scale, not a safety guarantee

These routes have different economics. A fund position requires capital; an affiliate arrangement begins with useful recommendations and an audience; a group property purchase shares ownership and funding. The videos do not establish that each produces income without maintenance or carries equivalent liquidity and loss risk.

Srivatsaa’s broader point is accumulation rather than graduation away from small holdings. TIGAs can be stacked as available capital grows. Their educational value lies in connecting an intentional allocation with the resulting ownership and income, rather than treating a small position as a shortcut to immediate financial independence.
