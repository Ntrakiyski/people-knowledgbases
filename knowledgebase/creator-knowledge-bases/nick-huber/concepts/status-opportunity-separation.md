## In I Got Mocked for Starting This Business

The account of moving boxes while peers pursued prestigious internships highlights a conflict between social status and a personally chosen business. The participant’s stated takeaway is that popularity or prestige is an unreliable reason by itself to choose a career.

> what's high status and the way everybody's going might not be the best way to go
