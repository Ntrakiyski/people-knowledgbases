These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Truth About Personal Branding

Writing and speaking regularly are presented as practice for clearer internal decisions and persuasion. The benefit extends beyond public distribution to messages to employees and vendors, although the speaker explicitly says such benefits are hard to measure.

> it's made me a much better communicator a much better leader when I can get my ideas across clearly in Slack
