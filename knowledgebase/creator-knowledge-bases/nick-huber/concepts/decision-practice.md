# Judgment needs opportunities to decide

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Why Most People Never Start

The speaker contrasts looking up predetermined answers with exercising responsibility in uncertain situations. The defensible teaching is to create practical decision experience; his sweeping description of education remains an opinion.

> so people don't get opportunities to make decisions people don't get opportunities to delegate

[Source summary](../sources/7_wq5V7-cB4.md); [raw transcript](../raw/7_wq5V7-cB4.md), characters 2503–2597.

## In If You Can't Make Decisions Don't Start a Business

The participant describes judgment as learned through feedback. Starting with lower stakes is a training suggestion, not an assurance that repetition always improves choices.

> the best way to start making decisions is when the consequences are very low and not when they're high

[Source summary](../sources/mKUlWUQqn9I.md); [raw transcript](../raw/mKUlWUQqn9I.md), characters 1440–1542.
