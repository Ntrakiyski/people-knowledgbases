# Hire when delivery crowds out growth work

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The First $100 Matters More Than Your Big Idea

The proposed trigger is being too busy to sell and develop the business. The first role may be administrative or operational depending on what consumes the founder's capacity.

> as soon as you are too busy to sell and grow your business and do the things that you need to do for future planning is when you make that first hire

[Source summary](../sources/H1GTRcbuSGg.md); [raw transcript](../raw/H1GTRcbuSGg.md), characters 1439–1588.

## In The Fastest Way To Grow Your Business

The proposed trigger is not being able to devote substantial time to selling and development. This interview uses a different percentage from other clips, so the numbers should be read as heuristics rather than a fixed rule.

> as soon as you can't spend half your time selling and doing business development it's time to hire

[Source summary](../sources/PTlE4pRylcs.md); [raw transcript](../raw/PTlE4pRylcs.md), characters 4470–4568.

## In You're Working Too Hard for Too Little

The suggested 25 percent allocation is the speaker's rule of thumb. It prompts a capacity review when a full workweek leaves no space for selling, recruiting, strategy, or development.

> as soon as you're not spending 25% of your working hours on things that are important but not urgent

[Source summary](../sources/elvH4as1Qzs.md); [raw transcript](../raw/elvH4as1Qzs.md), characters 579–679.
