## In Why Most People Never Start

The respondent worries that studying celebrated founders and planning far ahead can make ordinary paid work seem unworthy. This is an argument about attention and expectations, not proof that ambitious long-term goals are inherently counterproductive.

> I love the people who are super scrappy and get excited about a small what what on the outside looks like a small opportunity
