These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In The Hiring Secret That Changed My Business

The participant argues that strong candidates may already have satisfactory jobs and therefore never apply to an advertised vacancy. Active sourcing follows from that premise. The numerical estimates offered about workforce quality are unsupported claims in this conversation.

> you have to hunt for that talent you have to go find it and you have to go out and get it
