The participant groups business ideas by required skill, investment, and risk, then proposes a shortlist spanning three levels. Calling local competitors and asking about prices and availability is his suggested way to test apparent gaps. He strongly criticizes following passion, instead treating time as scarce capital to allocate toward a defined income goal.
