In this interview clip, Huber describes a choice between expanding a media operation and spending more time building operating businesses. His media employees also work inside the companies, so their cost is not presented as the cost of a standalone profitable publishing venture. He identifies two benefits: access to commercially relevant people, which need not require a large audience, and practice communicating clearly. Writing and speaking help him explain decisions to staff and vendors. He admires people who combine business building with a large public presence but does not claim that every founder should copy their staffing or expenditure.
