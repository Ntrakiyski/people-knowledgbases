Cory reports an HVAC-cleaning/duct-repair business at 1.25 million revenue and 479,000 profit with 60,000 debt. Because extra crews can handle more work, Alex treats demand as the constraint and recommends incremental improvements: a further price test, continued debt repayment, a dedicated mobile-friendly ad page, readable varied creative, then more spend if economics hold. Reactivation and HOA outreach follow, while a paid partner offer is discussed for later because reciprocal referrals do not necessarily scale. At follow-up Cory says leads rose from about 120 to nearly 200 monthly after changing agencies, pages and spend; he describes being on goal for 2.3–2.5 million and considers a future location.
