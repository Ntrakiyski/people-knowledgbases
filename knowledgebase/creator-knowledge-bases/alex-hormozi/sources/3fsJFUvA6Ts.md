# What Makes The Perfect Business (5 Things)

This page describes the selected source's claims and advice. The summary and interpretations are machine-extracted drafts; quotations have been matched against the exported transcript.

Alex describes five desirable business characteristics: repeat and expanding customer revenue; high prices relative to direct costs; a growing market; operational scalability with modest ongoing capital requirements; and differentiation. He distinguishes logo retention from cohort revenue retention and structural from voluntary churn. He treats the criteria as continua and cautions against abandoning a business after one video. Capital intensity can slow growth while deterring competitors, and brand can differentiate an otherwise commodity offer.

## Insights

### Distinguish retained customers from retained revenue

Logo retention tracks surviving customer accounts; net revenue retention also captures expansion and contraction in the original cohort. A rate above 100% is possible for revenue, not customer count.

## Context and limits

Portfolio revenue, cohort observations and category economics are reported, not independently verified. Later-tenure customers are selected survivors; their lower churn does not prove that reaching a particular month causes retention. Gross margin omits operating costs. Financing is never assured. The narration mixes patent, secret recipe and brand protection in the Coke example; these are different forms of intellectual property, and its specific protection claims are unverified.
