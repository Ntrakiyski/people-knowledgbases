The four-box framework distinguishes bootstrapping, raising equity, investing personal capital in others, and managing pooled outside capital. Bootstrapping preserves control but can create operational and technical compromises; fundraising enables capital-intensive opportunities while introducing dilution, preferences and investor incentives. Investing generally requires surplus earned elsewhere and patience. Fund management adds sourcing, risk, reporting and regulatory responsibilities. Alex describes having participated in all four, including privately raised real-estate deals.
