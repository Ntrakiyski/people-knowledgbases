# How To Make More Money Than You Deserve (My 2027 Framework)

Review scope: machine-extracted.

Alex Hormozi outlines a framework categorizing business models into distinct vehicle types based on operational difficulty and wealth potential. Labor-heavy commodity services and low-capital starter businesses offer initial cash flow or skill development but suffer from high operational friction and limited long-term scaling capacity. Personality-dependent or expert-driven services yield substantial immediate income yet prove difficult to transfer as independent enterprise assets. Defensible enterprises protected by regulatory hurdles, capital requirements, or complex skill moats create significant long-term enterprise value by limiting competitive entry. High-demand arbitrage opportunities, such as services tailored to aging demographics, commercial maintenance, and fund syndication, allow operators to achieve outsized financial returns with lower competitive resistance. Ultimately, opportunity vehicle selection constrains long-term financial growth more decisively than individual effort alone.
