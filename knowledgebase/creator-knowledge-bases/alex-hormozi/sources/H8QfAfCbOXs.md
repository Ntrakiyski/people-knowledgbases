Organizational culture is formed by spoken and unspoken feedback loops that govern how employee behaviors are rewarded or corrected. Rather than relying solely on executive promotions, leaders shape team culture horizontally by establishing minimum performance standards within each department. Tolerating mediocre work demotivates high performers and lowers organizational standards across the business. Establishing effective talent management involves streamlining termination processes for underperforming staff while assessing initial workplace output over subjective interview claims. Leaders who embrace the emotional discomfort of making difficult personnel decisions protect the broader organization, fostering an environment where exceptional talent can thrive.
