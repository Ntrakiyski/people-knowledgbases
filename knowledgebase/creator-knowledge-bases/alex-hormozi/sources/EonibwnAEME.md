Hormozi proposes six actions for people unsure what to do next: build general capacity, save money, acquire and practice skills, build an audience, develop a waiting list, and meet relevant people. The opening includes being rested and physically prepared, not only accumulating work hours. Later advice becomes more austere, including very low expenses, extensive use of nonwork hours, and spending surplus on skills. Audience and waiting-list activity are framed as potential leverage, not a fully formed product. The common thread is preparing before an opportunity arrives. The study story, financial biography, and implied inevitability of audience monetization are not verified.
