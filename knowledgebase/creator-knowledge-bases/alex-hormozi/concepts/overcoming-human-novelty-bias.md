These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In 3 signs you're going to be a successful entrepreneur.

Alex explains that instinctual desires for instant results and constant novelty hinder business growth. Achieving commercial success requires resisting shiny distractions and consistently executing fundamental tasks over long periods.

> we as humans want new stuff we want it now and we want it to be easy and those three things are actually the antithesis of what is required
