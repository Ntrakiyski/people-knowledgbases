# Retaining options indefinitely prevents meaningful execution

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Change My Mind, Win $10,000

The speaker suggests that attempting to keep every strategic option open leads to indecision and missed opportunities. Achieving substantial business or personal milestones requires making explicit trade-offs and committing fully to a single path.

> in their pursuit of trying to keep every option open they actually close all their doors because they're unwilling to commit to one
