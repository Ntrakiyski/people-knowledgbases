## In Change My Mind, Win $10,000

The speaker highlights that hiring fewer high-performing employees at higher compensation yields greater output and lower management friction than employing multiple average workers, provided the business is capitalized to retain them.

> pay better people better and it will cost you less than paying poor people poorly
