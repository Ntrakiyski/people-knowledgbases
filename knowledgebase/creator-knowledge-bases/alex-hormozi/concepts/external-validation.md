# Adopting external success metrics creates mediocre outcomes

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In a video to watch if you're ambitious and in your 20s or 30s

The speaker observes that many individuals live unfulfilling lives by accepting success criteria established by others. Pursuing goals designed by external parties frequently requires trade-offs that undermine personal well-being and long-term career satisfaction.

> so many people live mediocre lives because they play games that other people designed that even if they did win they wouldn't want the prize
