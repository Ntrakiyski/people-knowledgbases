# Brand partnerships depend on customer segment alignment

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How I Would Build a Personal Brand in 2027 (If I Had to Start Over)

The speaker emphasizes that the commercial value of a brand association depends on the preferences of a specific target market. Partnering with entities that alienate core customers can reduce revenue and damage market position.

> there's no inherently good or bad association it's just is it a good association for the ideal customer that you have
