These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In 3 signs you're going to be a successful entrepreneur.

Alex notes that taking impulsive action often serves to relieve internal anxiety rather than address genuine business needs. Leaders must tolerate temporary discomfort and allow emotional reactions to pass before altering strategic direction.

> taking action often times does alleviate anxiety but it doesn't mean that the action was still correct
