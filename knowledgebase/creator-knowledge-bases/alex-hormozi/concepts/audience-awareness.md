## In Building a $3,000,000 Business for a Stranger in 31 Minutes &#124; Scale or Fail

Alex Hormozi explains that ad performance degrades when campaigns rely exclusively on warm, product-aware audiences. Expanding ad volume requires testing hooks focused on general pain points to attract broader prospective client segments.

> So your advertising should speak to them differently He contends that audiences go from the bottom which is the warmest and smallest group
