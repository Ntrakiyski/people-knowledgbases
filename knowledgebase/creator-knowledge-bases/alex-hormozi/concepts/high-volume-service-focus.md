# Prioritize high-volume service over low-frequency overhauls

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How I Would Build a $10M Service Business (If I Had to Start Over)

Alex advises that focusing on high-volume repair services builds operational stability and reliable cash flow. Lowering transaction friction shortens sales cycles, allowing sales representatives to gain frequent repetitions. While major overhauls offer larger individual transactions, neglected smaller repairs represent a high-margin market with reduced competition.

> no one wants to do this particular thing but then you orient a business in such a way that you can actually make really good gross profits on it
