# Operational difficulty creates competitive moats

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How To Make More Money Than You Deserve (My 2027 Framework)

The speaker suggests that high entry barriers, such as regulatory compliance, capital demands, or specialized technical skills, reduce market competition. Facing structural obstacles allows persistent operators to build highly defensible enterprises with reduced competitive crowding.

> and the non-obvious truth is that the difficulty can be a competitive advantage and so said differently if everyone can do it everyone will do it
