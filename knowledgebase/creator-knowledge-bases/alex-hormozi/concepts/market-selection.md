These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Change My Mind, Win $10,000

The speaker advises entrepreneurs to sell to affluent customer segments with higher willingness to pay. Attempting to serve low-income customer segments requires substantial upfront capital reserves to sustain high-volume, low-margin business operations.

> if you're a lowcost leader you have to make it up in volume in order to make it up in volume you usually have to sustain more cost up front
