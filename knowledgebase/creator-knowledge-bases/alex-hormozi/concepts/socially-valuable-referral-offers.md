## In $100M Worth of Marketing Knowledge in 32 Minutes &#124; Scale or Fail

Alex suggests designing referral programs that grant existing clients high-value passes to share with acquaintances. Offering substantial experiences rather than minor discounts gives clients a compelling reason to introduce friends.

> you want every referral to have a great reason to tell a friend
