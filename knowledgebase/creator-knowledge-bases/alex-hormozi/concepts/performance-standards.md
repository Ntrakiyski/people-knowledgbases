## In If I could start over in business, I’d do this sooner

The speaker notes that team culture is established by the lowest level of acceptable performance within a department. Removing underperforming staff signals clear expectations and establishes trust with high-performing employees.

> one of the easiest things that you can do is remove the people who suck because the culture is built from the bottom up
