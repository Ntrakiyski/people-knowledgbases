# Sales proposals must align with buyer incentives

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In 8 Entrepreneurs Compete For $100,000

The speaker explains that effective sales messaging requires reverse engineering the primary desires and criteria of the prospect. When pitching investors or clients, presenting solutions that directly address buyer priorities yields higher deal conversion rates.

> whenever I'm selling anything I always have to start with what is you reverse engineer from the prospect
