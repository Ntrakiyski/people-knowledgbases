# Public view metrics provide misleading business signals

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to become rich with social media (my exact playbook)

The speaker argues that social media algorithms optimize for broad entertainment appeal rather than commercial buying intent. Relying on view counts as a primary metric can lead creators to produce content that attracts viewers while depressing total revenue.

> the algorithm will give you the wrong signal for your business And so the algorithm will tell you what the most people like not the most valuable people like
