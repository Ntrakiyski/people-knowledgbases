These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In 3 signs you're going to be a successful entrepreneur.

Alex references psychological paradoxes showing that individuals provide far better advice to others than to themselves. Removing personal emotional attachment allows founders to view operational challenges objectively and select logical solutions.

> you try and give someone else advice you give way better advice than you follow for yourself
