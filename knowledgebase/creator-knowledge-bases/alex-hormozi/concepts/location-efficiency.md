# Optimizing existing facility capacity precedes multi-location expansion

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In This Video Should Be Required Viewing for Business Owners

An advisor warns against expanding into additional physical locations before fully optimizing existing facilities. Maximizing throughput and profitability within a single operational site generates higher returns with lower capital expenditure than opening unproven new branches.

> a lot of people think they need to scale to make more money but usually you need to nail it to make more money
