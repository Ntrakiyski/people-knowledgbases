## In How to Get Rich for Service-Based Businesses (Thank Me Later)

Alex advises service owners to build dynamic cost models that calculate exact project expenses before quoting clients. Standardizing pricing rules prevents arbitrary estimates, shortens sales delays, and protects gross profit margins.
