## Context and limits

Educational approximations contain errors. $1.5m revenue at 30% is $450k, not $500k. He verbally says cash minus burn for runway, while his worked $100k/$20k example correctly divides to get five months. Revenue/churn LTV omits margin, discounting and varying retention; gross margin differs from net cash. A 70% margin or 3% monthly churn is not a universal target. Simplified profit multiples do not determine actual enterprise/equity value, debt or transaction proceeds.

## What the complete reading adds

Adds formula and arithmetic qualifications, distinguishes gross profit from cash and treats industry-wide benchmarks as personal heuristics rather than rules.
