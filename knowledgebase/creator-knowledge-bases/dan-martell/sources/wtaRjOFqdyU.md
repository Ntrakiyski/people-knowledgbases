# How to Make AI Sound EXACTLY Like you

This page describes the selected source's claims and advice. The summary and interpretations are machine-extracted drafts; quotations have been matched against the exported transcript.

Review scope: machine-extracted.

Generating authentic, personalized content with artificial intelligence requires constructing a central repository of recorded thoughts, meeting transcripts, and past writings. Analyzing these inputs allows creators to generate tone style guides that capture distinct phrasing, sentence structures, and banned vocabulary. Connecting software tools directly to email and communication channels enables drafting messages in a personalized voice. However, creators must review all automated drafts to preserve brand credibility. Ultimately, artificial intelligence should assist ideation and drafting while human judgment retains control over final publishing decisions.
