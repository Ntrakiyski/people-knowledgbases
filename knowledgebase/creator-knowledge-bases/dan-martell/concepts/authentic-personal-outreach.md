## In How to Make Rich Friends

A speaker cautions against using automated scripts or software bots when reaching out to prospective mentors. Conducting genuine, researched personal outreach demonstrates sincere respect and distinguishes the sender from generic spam.

> Do not use AI to do these outreaches You can use it for inspiration but you need to actually do it yourself
