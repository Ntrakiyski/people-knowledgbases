## In If I Wanted to Make My First $100K/Month, I’d Do This

The speaker proposes marketing and calls before building an offering with a long lead time. A waitlist can help test expressed interest while limiting premature delivery work. Expressed interest is not a completed sale, and pre-launch validation does not eliminate execution or financial risk.
