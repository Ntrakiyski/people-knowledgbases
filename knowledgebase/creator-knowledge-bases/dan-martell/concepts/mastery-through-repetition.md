# Sustain deliberate practice until flawless execution becomes instinctual

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How To Become Dangerously Self-Educated (with AI)

The speaker advocates repeated deliberate practice aimed at reliable execution rather than one-time exposure to an idea. This is a training recommendation. It does not establish that any amount of practice makes errors impossible or guarantees mastery.

> they get it once and then they move on but the pros they practice until they can't get it wrong
