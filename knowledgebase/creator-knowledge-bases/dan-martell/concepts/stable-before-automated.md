# Stabilize a process before trying to automate it

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In 40 Hacks to Use AI So Well It Feels Unfair

A frequently changing process is called a moving target. The same source acknowledges that rapidly growing companies may legitimately change processes, so stability is a selection criterion rather than a universal freeze.

> If your process changes every week there's nothing stable to automate
