# Generate authentic goodwill by providing value without transactional tracking

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Make Rich Friends

A speaker asserts that enduring professional goodwill accrues when leaders provide assistance without keeping score. Giving value upfront through encouragement, resources, or support establishes trust and attracts high-quality partners naturally.

> goodwill is only created when you give more than what you take off the table
