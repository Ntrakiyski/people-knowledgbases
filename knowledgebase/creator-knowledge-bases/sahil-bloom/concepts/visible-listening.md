# Show engagement while the other person speaks

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How to Turn Small Talk Into Deep Conversation (Psychology)

Brief responses and body language are proposed to make listening perceptible. Visible performance should not be mistaken for proof of comprehension.

> show the other person that you are listening with your actions body language or with simple sounds
