# Consider product familiarity when selecting commercial partners

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How I actually make money (author, investor, founder)

The narrator says he approaches companies whose products he already uses and prefers long-term partnerships. He also acknowledges that choosing selectively is easier in an established position. Product familiarity is his stated selection criterion, not independent evidence of product quality.

> companies that I've been an individual personal fan and user of full price paying customer of for an extended period of time
