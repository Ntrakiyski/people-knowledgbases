# Separate a headline contract value from cash-payment timing

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How I actually make money (author, investor, founder)

The narrator explains that his book advances are spread across milestones and years. This distinguishes the announced size of an agreement from cash arriving immediately. The payment structure described should not be assumed to apply to every publishing contract.

> even when you hear seven figure book deal the actual payments are spread out over the course of 2 three maybe even four years
