# Use small completions as a short reset when overwhelmed

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How I actually make money (author, investor, founder)

When describing an overwhelmed day, the narrator temporarily starts with short tasks to build momentum before larger work. The context matters: this is a reset tactic, not a claim that small tasks should always outrank consequential work.

> when I'm feeling overwhelmed I actually flip that on its head and I start with the smallest things first
