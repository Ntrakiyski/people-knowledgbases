Using the tragedy-of-the-commons analogy, Bloom separates nations, AI builders, adopting businesses and individuals. He argues each may rationally race for advantage while jointly creating unemployment, inequality, malicious use and lost meaning; he additionally entertains loss-of-control scenarios. He proposes lowering the cost of long-term thinking while recognizing possible major AI benefits. He invites criticism rather than offering a concrete regulatory design.
