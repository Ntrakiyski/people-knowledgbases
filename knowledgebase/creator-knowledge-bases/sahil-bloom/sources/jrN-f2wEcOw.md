The game of threes asks what friends request help with, what one does better than peers, and what one does for free. Bloom then recommends talking to people to find paid problems those skills can solve and adding complementary capabilities as needs grow. Four supporting gears cover confidence through action, an initial client, value-led networking, and managing money. His suggested allocation splits surplus between cash savings and index funds until a stated reserve target, then directs surplus toward investing. The ending explicitly says the process takes patience and consistency, qualifying the opening promise to end money worries.
