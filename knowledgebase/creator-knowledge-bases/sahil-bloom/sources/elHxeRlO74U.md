Bloom presents four conversational practices. Doorknob questions invite stories rather than short factual answers; visible listening uses responses and body language to show interest; repeat-and-follow paraphrases what was heard before adding a question or thought; and situational eye contact changes with who is speaking. The examples concern both personal and professional relationships. The video credits loud listening to David Brooks and promotes Bloom's book. It does not demonstrate that the practices work uniformly across cultures, disabilities, or conversational preferences.
