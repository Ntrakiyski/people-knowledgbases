Bloom names five difficult aspects of entrepreneurship: the responsibility attached to freedom, institutions designed around regular employment, loss of imposed structure, loneliness, and exaggerated concern about other people's judgment. He explicitly rejects the claim that everyone should become an entrepreneur. His proposed responses include accepting responsibility, creating even loose time blocks, allowing time to build new supportive relationships, and acting by one's own values. The video's candid qualifications coexist with broad generalizations about loneliness and other people's insecurity. It is an attributed personal account, not a population-wide assessment of entrepreneurial wellbeing.
