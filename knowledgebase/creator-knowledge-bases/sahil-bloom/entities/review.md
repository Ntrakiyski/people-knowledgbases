# Entity context review

This guide interprets selected named references in the forty-video sample. Codex reviewed the cited notes and original passages. The [mention index](index.md) is not an exhaustive biography, relationship graph, or independent verification of the creator's claims.

**The Five Types of Wealth is both teaching context and an offer.** The source material uses life-design frameworks while directing viewers to the book. Launch vlogs add self-reported sales milestones and emotional experience. A promotion and a claimed result should remain distinguishable. See [conversation advice and book promotion](../sources/elHxeRlO74U.md) and [the bestseller account](../sources/vvMaFyr_214.md).

**Richard Branson is part of a reported event setting.** The retreat source says the creator is co-hosting at Branson's island, then offers lessons about intuition, work, and changing course. This is not a comprehensive statement of Branson's beliefs or independent verification of the event. See [the retreat reflections](../sources/gP3QmGssISw.md).

**Public figures can be quoted illustrations.** Bezos appears in an argument about asking what remains stable. The quotation is evidenced by Sahil's transcript, not checked against the original interview. See [the lighthouse framework](../sources/JcfSxUg6dH0.md).

**Technology companies appear as actors in a scenario.** The AI commons discussion names builders and other institutions while discussing incentives. Their presence does not establish endorsement or predicted conduct as fact. See [the collective-action argument](../sources/AHb_AFtC-Yk.md).

**Products in vlogs may have commercial context.** The income and travel sources include partnerships and product promotion alongside personal routines. Reported skincare, supplement, and performance effects remain unverified. See [income and partnership choices](../sources/WpXndHBtvCQ.md) and [the travel vlog](../sources/y1fK-0oDG7I.md).

Literal matching can omit shortened or misspelled names. First mentions alone cannot establish the role of every entity throughout a video.
