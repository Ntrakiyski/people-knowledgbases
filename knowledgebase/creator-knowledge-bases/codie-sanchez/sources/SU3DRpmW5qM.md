The wealth-elevator metaphor starts with financial literacy, manual expense tracking and automated low-cost investing. Lower floors emphasize survival costs, emergency reserves, skills and expensive-debt payoff, including avalanche and snowball options. Later floors add employer matches, retirement and taxable investments, minority ownership, conditional alternatives and delegation. Full ownership is proposed after investment cash flow covers living costs; the upper level mixes concentrated business attention, other streams, preservation and legacy.
