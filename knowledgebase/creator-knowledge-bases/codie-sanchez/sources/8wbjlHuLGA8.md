Mastering sales operations requires shifting from needy, wallet-constrained thinking to confident problem-solving. Sellers must avoid underpricing their offerings or projecting personal financial limitations onto prospective buyers, recognizing that solutions derive value from the magnitude of problems solved. Effective sales conversations involve asking structured questions that guide prospects to articulate their own gap between current challenges and desired outcomes.
