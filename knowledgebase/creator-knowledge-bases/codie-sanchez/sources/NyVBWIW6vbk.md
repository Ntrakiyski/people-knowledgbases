## Context and limits

Dollar-cost averaging is a contribution method, whereas concentration concerns the chosen assets; the title conflates them. Startup private rounds, institutional PE holdings, and small owner-operated businesses have different risks and access. Illiquidity, leverage, operating work, financing payments, taxes, and failed acquisitions are not comparably modeled. The example of three businesses and aggregate revenue is ambiguous about the revenue base; it does not support a precise net owner-income comparison. Forecasts and event urgency are promotional claims, not verified market timing.
