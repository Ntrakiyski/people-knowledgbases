## Context and limits

This is Sanchez's narrated interpretation, not a Huizenga interview. The assertion that ninety acquisitions over four years equals one every three days is arithmetically inconsistent: the interval is roughly sixteen days. The narration discusses post-IPO activity before saying 'then came' the IPO, so order of presentation is not reliable chronology. Stock consideration can dilute ownership and transfer risk; describing it as paper without cash cost does not make it economically free.
