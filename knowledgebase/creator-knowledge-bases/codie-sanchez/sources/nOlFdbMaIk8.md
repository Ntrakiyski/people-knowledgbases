Using business anecdotes and behavioral claims, Sanchez argues repeated small choices accumulate. Her MICRO sequence is notice the moment, identify the lever, constrain or cut, reinforce results, and offload. Examples include agendas, email boundaries, meeting batching, automatic savings, onboarding recordings, meal preparation, dictation and assistants. She shows AI-built dashboard and meeting-tracking examples and promotes a domain provider.
