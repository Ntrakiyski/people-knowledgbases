A speaker explains how sophisticated investors leverage debt as a tool for wealth creation rather than a financial burden. Borrowing against existing portfolio assets allows individuals to access liquidity without triggering taxable sales or relinquishing asset growth. In business acquisitions, utilizing seller financing and government-backed small business loans enables buyers to acquire cash-flowing enterprises with minimal upfront capital. However, borrowers must manage leverage conservatively to prevent liquidity crises during market downturns.
