## In Everything They Teach You At Goldman Sachs

A speaker observes that attempting to conceal performance gaps destroys professional trust. Owning setbacks transparently and presenting corrective action plans builds lasting credibility with partners and clients.

> people are actually going to have more respect for you when you say "I'm failing at this here's why here's what I'm doing about it."
