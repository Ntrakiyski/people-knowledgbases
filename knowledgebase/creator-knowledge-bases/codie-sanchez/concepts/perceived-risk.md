## In 20 Years Of Money Advice in 22 Minutes (How I Made Millions)

Codie Sanchez observes that pursuing ambitious career leaps often appears unnecessarily risky to comfortable observers. Taking calculated risks remains essential for achieving substantial financial growth.

> risk always looks reckless to the people standing still it only stops looking crazy once you can start lifting them with you
