## In There Are Only 3 Types Of Profitable Businesses (Pick ONE)

Codie Sanchez notes that lifestyle businesses should be evaluated by owner happiness and personal time autonomy rather than market share expansion. Maintaining clear boundaries prevents lifestyle operations from incurring unnecessary complexity.

> the goal of this business is really one question are you happy and if not then this business probably isn't for you
