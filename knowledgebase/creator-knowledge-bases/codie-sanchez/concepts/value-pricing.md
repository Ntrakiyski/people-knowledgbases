# Raising prices filters out price sensitive customers

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In How To Grow Your Business SO Fast It Feels Illegal

The speaker argues that undercharging attracts demanding clients who leave when cheaper options appear. Increasing prices allows businesses to filter for loyal customers who prioritize overall value over lowest cost.

> By raising prices you actually filter all those people out You find your fans Then they'll say tell me if this is you thinking in your head
