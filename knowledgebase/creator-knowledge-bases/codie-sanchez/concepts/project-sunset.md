# Terminating unviable projects prevents ongoing resource drain

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In $100M CEO Explains: 7 Habits That Hold Entrepreneurs Back

Codie Sanchez emphasizes that setting clear evaluation deadlines for new initiatives prevents prolonged investment in underperforming ideas. Killing weak projects quickly frees up capital for higher-performing opportunities.

> killing an idea doesn't mean you're a failure it means you are pushing forward and just found a path that didn't work out
