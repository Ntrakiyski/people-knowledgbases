## In Forget MBAs, THIS is The Fastest Path to Wealth

The Japan story is used to argue that profitable firms need successors willing and able to run them. The geographic analogy is a motivating claim, not evidence that local businesses can generally be acquired free.

> every profitable business can continue to live on if you actually learn how to take up the baton
