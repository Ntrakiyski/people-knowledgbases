These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In Unfortunately, Sales Really Is This Simple (4 Steps)

Codie Sanchez warns against evaluating service prices against one's personal bank account. Pricing should reflect the commercial value of the problem being solved for the customer.

> do not sell based on what is in your wallet you are not rich enough
