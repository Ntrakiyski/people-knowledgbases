## In 20 Years Of Money Advice in 22 Minutes (How I Made Millions)

Codie Sanchez argues that remote work prioritizes short-term comfort over rapid professional development. Being physically present with high performers fosters spontaneous opportunities and accelerates career progression.

> remote work optimizes for your comfort growth is not about comfort and you don't get that friction that difficulty from sitting on your couch
