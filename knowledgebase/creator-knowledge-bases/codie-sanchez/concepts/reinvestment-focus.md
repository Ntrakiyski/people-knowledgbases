## In There Are Only 3 Types Of Profitable Businesses (Pick ONE)

Codie Sanchez explains that scaling an enterprise requires reinvesting nearly all generated profits back into infrastructure, technology, and talent. Founders seeking maximum personal income early should avoid scale models.

> reinvest nearly everything in the business to grow it as fast as humanly possible
