These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In $100M CEO Explains: 7 Habits That Hold Entrepreneurs Back

Codie Sanchez highlights research showing that brief task interruptions require significant time for individuals to regain deep focus. Structuring work into dedicated time blocks prevents constant schedule fragmentation.

> when you are interrupted it takes you on average 23 minutes to refocus on a new task
