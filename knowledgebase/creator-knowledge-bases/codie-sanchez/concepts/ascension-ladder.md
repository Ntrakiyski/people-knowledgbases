# Structuring tiered service progression drives natural client expansion

These source notes collect the treatments found so far. They preserve differences between videos; a reviewed cross-video synthesis is pending.

## In I Built A Million Dollar Business For A Subscriber in 23 Minutes

Codie Sanchez advocates for creating an ascending ladder of service packages ranging from entry-level offerings to premium recurring tiers. Providing a clear upgrade path enables existing clients to expand their commitments naturally over time.

> There should be a natural progression from the things you do that are the cheapest to the things you do that are the most expensive
