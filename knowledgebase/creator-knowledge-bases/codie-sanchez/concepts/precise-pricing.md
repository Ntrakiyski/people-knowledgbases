## In The Psychology of Sales (How We Sell $100M)

Codie Sanchez notes that using precise, non-rounded figures during pricing proposals increases client trust. Specific numbers suggest rigorous cost calculation, whereas rounded estimates often invite price pushback.

> stop using round numbers fascinating studies showing don't quote 450K quote 437,500
