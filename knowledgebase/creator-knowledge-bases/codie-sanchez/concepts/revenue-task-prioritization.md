## In 7 Habits That Made Me Rich (But They're Weird)

A speaker recommends writing down the primary task that directly generates revenue every morning before checking phone messages. Focusing on primary financial levers prevents passive task completion from substituting for progress.

> every morning before you pick up your phone I want you to write one line The one task that if done and done only it actually makes you money
