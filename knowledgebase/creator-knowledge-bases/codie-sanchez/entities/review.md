# Entity context review

This guide reviews selected entity roles in the forty-video sample. The [name index](index.md) remains literal and curated. Codex reviewed the cited source notes and passages; identities, financial results, and affiliations have not been independently verified.

**Books and business education are commercial context.** Main Street Millionaire is invoked while explaining service systems and expertise. Workshop and boardroom references are part of the content's route into paid education, not evidence that the underlying strategy works for every buyer. See [the affluent-service framework](../sources/olaz2NJeUUI.md) and [incentives and workshop context](../sources/mseVr7PBOl0.md).

**A biographical example is the creator's account.** The Wayne Huizenga narrative is organized around systems, capital, people, and ownership incentives. Its transaction values and historical claims need separate sources before reuse as facts. See [the leverage narrative](../sources/pD7TO-Pwlzk.md).

**A platform mention has multiple possible roles.** Google can mean advertising, local search, a source of business leads, or a corporation used as an example. YouTube and Instagram can be distribution channels or incidental narration. Do not collapse these into endorsements. Compare [an AI operating experiment](../sources/yO5O_ngyA_8.md) with [media distribution](../sources/ZvAwqGH_79Q.md).

**Financial institutions and government programs need context.** An employment anecdote is not a present affiliation. A claimed loan, grant, or tax provision is not verified eligibility. See [career and spending context](../sources/yveLqk3DCNs.md) and [the incentive discussion](../sources/mseVr7PBOl0.md).

The index records first literal matches, not all references or resolved relationships. Unrecognized names and caption errors remain outside that guarantee.
