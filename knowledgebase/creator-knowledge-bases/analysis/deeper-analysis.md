## What changes the interpretation

That allocation tension remains unresolved. The [trailer](../alex-hormozi/sources/Z5rKn1ZIp3A.md) establishes a competition format, not a successful intervention.

### Codie: private ownership is a thesis with commercial context

Despite its title, the [index-investing critique](../codie-sanchez/sources/NyVBWIW6vbk.md) calls inexpensive index funds a reasonable default and explicitly describes acquisitions as slow, careful work requiring diligence. It moves among venture investing, institutional private equity, and ordinary local ownership. Those are different exposures and operating commitments. Seller financing and equity consideration alter how a purchase is funded; neither makes a business free.

The [MBA critique](../codie-sanchez/sources/crMQX981PYU.md) acknowledges that her own MBA opened doors while promoting her academy. The [Huizenga story](../codie-sanchez/sources/pD7TO-Pwlzk.md) offers useful operating ideas but contains an internal arithmetic problem: ninety acquisitions over four years is roughly one every sixteen days, not one every three days. This check identifies an inconsistency in the narration; it does not independently verify the acquisition count or biography.

The full [daily routine](../codie-sanchez/sources/yBefMa8LydA.md) includes sleep, protected evenings, recovery, and spouse boundaries. It should not be summarized solely as doing more in every available minute.

## Use these distinctions in later analysis

Compare recommendations at the same stage and with the same objective. An employee building skills, an owner operating a leveraged acquisition, and a wealthy investor allocating surplus are solving different problems. A goal of reinvesting time in growth differs from a goal of protecting family time, even when both use delegation and calendar audits.

Keep the status of each statement attached: proposed action, hypothetical example, personal report, forecast, commercial claim, or independently checked result. An exact transcript quotation verifies what the export says; it does not establish that the claim is true. None of these deeper readings provides independent verification of creator results, health claims, investment returns, or software performance.

## What the final readings add

### Alex: qualify the growth claim and the owner's objective

The [railing case](../alex-hormozi/sources/HGZOxBfnF-E.md) includes an owner follow-up: revenue rises from $2.5m to $3.6m and profit from $384k to $540k. That is reported growth of 44% and about 40.6%, respectively, with margins near 15%. It is a useful outcome account, but below the earlier 3–4× projection; the reported 20% close rate is also below the illustrative 50–60% expectation. Attribution repair, offer changes, pricing and campaign changes occur together, preventing isolation of one cause.

The [cleaning case](../alex-hormozi/sources/OQf2Ba-Lp_4.md) is weaker evidence for realized revenue than its title implies. The owner says he is on goal for $2.3–2.5m after a $1.25m baseline and reports monthly leads moving from about 120 to nearly 200. No updated profit or confirmation of paid-off debt is supplied. The narration's “almost doubled” should not replace that narrower account. Suggested allergy reductions and energy savings are hypothetical marketing examples, not demonstrated results.

The [six-owner coaching session](../alex-hormozi/sources/0EqJD2o-Mnk.md) accepts protecting family time and reducing current profit to hire leadership. It also recommends scaling existing networking before unfamiliar channels and distinguishes passive investments from active distractions. Its advice is conditional on the owner and constraint. A forceful recommendation to exit construction for elevators is explicitly more aggressive than the alternative of hiring leaders.

The [moving-company competition](../alex-hormozi/sources/zzleYxkf39k.md) works through offer → sales → referral partner → traffic. The “fail” verdict is about suitability for a year of further help, not an observed business failure. Internally named mover levels are not external certification. Rehearsed competitor-risk statistics are not independently established facts. This source is valuable for studying persuasive sales construction and the evidentiary limits of that persuasion.

The [business-quality framework](../alex-hormozi/sources/3fsJFUvA6Ts.md) treats retention, margins, market direction, scalability and defensibility as continua. Capital can be both a growth burden and a competitive barrier. The [e-commerce session](../alex-hormozi/sources/8C_6qojTA78.md) adds the product's role in fulfilling a brand promise, inventory cash requirements and observable sales training. It also shows Alex revising his logistics diagnosis after learning more about the owner's actual process.

## A framework for the user's later analysis

A useful organizing hypothesis across these creators is: choose a worthwhile outcome; identify demand and delivery constraints; prove value manually; make the process repeatable; delegate or automate it; expand distribution while measuring cash and quality. This sequence is an editorial comparison, not something all five explicitly agree on. Sahil contributes a prominent test of the outcome's personal cost; Nick emphasizes operational responsibility; Dan emphasizes capacity and workflow; Codie emphasizes ownership and deal structure; Alex emphasizes offers, conversion and business constraints.

For each later question, attach eight fields to the claim: actual speaker, business/life stage, objective, proposed action, required resources, evidence type, commercial interest and unresolved limitation. Compare the same outcome at the same stage. A tactic for an owner with a staff and substantial free cash is not automatically a transition plan for an employee with dependants.

Evidence types should remain distinct: principle; proposed intervention; hypothetical calculation; personal retrospective; reported follow-up; forecast; independently verified result. This pass adds complete readings and source-grounded interpretation, not the last category. Exact quotes have character offsets, with no invented timestamps. Whole-video qualifications remain in [video-reviews.jsonl](video-reviews.jsonl), and the underlying quotes remain in [insights.jsonl](insights.jsonl).
