# ChatGPT's Deal Can Make You a Millionaire (Here's How)

Sharran presents an account of OpenAI hiring OpenClaw creator Peter Steinberger while leaving the open-source project independent. He frames the strategic rationale as competition shifting from chatbots toward agents, and argues that a deal can target a creator’s attention and capabilities without acquiring a codebase. He analyzes terms, capital, and risks through Deal DNA. Crucially, he explicitly says the financial details and capital stack are not public, so compensation estimates, participation units, investor allocations, and other reconstructed terms remain speculation within the video.

The general teaching is his speed formula: recognize an opportunity, make a workable plan, execute, and compress the time between those steps. Flexibility about the other party’s nonnegotiable goals can matter more than offering the highest price. He also discusses compute costs, regulatory scrutiny, open-source competition, and talent departure as risks. Viewers are encouraged to identify a market gap, prepare useful skills, ask what must be true for success, and adapt their plan as new information arrives; millionaire outcomes are promotional possibilities, not demonstrated guarantees.

## Concepts covered

- [Acquihire](../concepts/acquihire.md): A deal can prioritize acquiring a team’s capabilities and attention over buying its product.
- [Deal structure](../concepts/deal-structure.md): A transaction’s terms distribute control, ownership, obligations, and future upside.
- [Risk Management](../concepts/risk-management.md): Evaluate downside, obligations, behavioral errors, and execution dependence before and after committing resources.
- [Skill Development](../concepts/skill-development.md): Acquire and combine useful capabilities through focused learning, practice, and real application.
- [Speed formula](../concepts/speed-formula.md): Move from opportunity to plan to action while minimizing unnecessary delay.
