# Millionaire Advice I'm Giving My Kids I Wish I Knew in My 20s

Sharran explains how he tries to teach his children to create value rather than depend on allowances. He separates unpaid household responsibilities from useful paid work, recommends praising effort, and uses a real e-commerce business and his son’s paid ghostwriting as examples. He also advocates a child-benefit trust owning an LLC, with parent-paid grantor-trust taxes and documented earnings; the legal, eligibility, and annual-gift statements are his claims rather than verified universal rules.

Later steps focus on accessing capital without selling investments, converting earnings into ownership, and involving children in giving. He describes whole-life policy loans, a land deal, cash-management alternatives, and long-term investment conversations. His five-year doubling illustration assumes repeated doubling rather than establishing expected returns. The final step uses a donor-advised fund and family charity discussions to frame generational wealth as learned judgment and generosity as well as assets.

## Concepts covered

- [Donor-advised giving](../concepts/donor-advised-giving.md): A dedicated giving account used to separate contribution timing from later charity selection.
- [Financial education for children](../concepts/financial-education-for-children.md): Teaching value creation, investing, and stewardship through real economic participation.
- [Invest First](../concepts/invest-first.md): Convert a deliberate share of income into assets before expanding lifestyle spending.
- [Skill Development](../concepts/skill-development.md): Acquire and combine useful capabilities through focused learning, practice, and real application.
- [Whole-life policy borrowing](../concepts/whole-life-banking.md): Using a permanent insurance policy’s accumulated cash value as collateral for loans.
