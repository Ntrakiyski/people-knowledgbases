# Once I Hit These Money Milestones, I Became Financially Free

Srivatsaa organizes wealth building into the MONEY framework: margin, optionality, net assets, equity rich, and living life on your terms. He begins with active income exceeding monthly expenses, then recommends a transition fund covering six to twelve months of spending so income loss does not force an immediate decision. His $24,000 reserve example covers six months of $4,000 expenses. He suggests high yield savings or stocks for reserves, without examining the latter's short term market risk.

He then targets $100,000 of net financial assets, using hypothetical 10% annual growth to illustrate compounding, and advocates freezing lifestyle spending as earnings rise. His definitions move between home equity and liquid financial assets, a distinction readers should preserve. He describes $1 million of net worth excluding a primary residence as an accredited investor milestone opening private deals, and claims these outperform retail investing by 54%, without supplying evidence here. Finally, he defines freedom as passive income exceeding expenses, explains income prefunded with capital or effort, and shows how increasing both invested assets and their yield can raise cash flow. These thresholds and comparisons are his framework rather than verified universal outcomes.

## Concepts covered

- [Accredited Investing](../concepts/accredited-investing.md): Investor eligibility can expand access to private market offerings.
- [Compound Growth](../concepts/compound-growth.md): Returns can earn further returns as an invested balance grows over time.
- [Financial Freedom](../concepts/financial-freedom.md): Financial freedom means recurring passive income covers spending and makes work optional.
- [Income Margin](../concepts/income-margin.md): The surplus between earnings and spending that funds future assets and financial resilience.
- [Passive Income](../concepts/passive-income.md): Income prefunded with money or effort can reduce dependence on ongoing paid work.
- [Transition Fund](../concepts/transition-fund.md): A reserve covering months of expenses buys time to replace or reinvent active income.
