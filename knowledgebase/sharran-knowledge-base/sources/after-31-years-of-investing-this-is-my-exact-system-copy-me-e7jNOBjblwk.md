# After 31 Years of Investing, THIS Is My Exact System (Copy Me)

Srivatsaa organizes his investing system into rules, strategy, and cadence. His rules retain at least 20% in opportunistic cash, limit a position to 5% and a theme to 10%, and favor investments with more than one purpose. Pair trades illustrate relative conviction; harvesting a declining investment’s loss illustrates a second tax purpose. The account contains unresolved sizing inconsistencies: he calls the 5% cap a maximum of twenty stocks and later says he holds seven to ten positions, without explaining how those statements reconcile with an otherwise invested portfolio.

Strategy begins by choosing whether one is a professional active investor, a thematic investor, or a passive investor delegating work to managers. His definition of passive investing is narrower than common usage. He distinguishes asset allocation, the investment mix, from asset location, the jurisdiction and account holding each asset, and favors placing frequent trading in tax advantaged accounts. His claims about no taxes and indefinite loss carryforwards require account, jurisdiction, and rule specific qualification. Finally, weekly position checks, monthly trade reviews, and quarterly loss harvesting are intended to make decisions systematic and calm. The video offers the speaker’s personal process, not proof that pairs eliminate market risk or that brief reviews safely govern every options strategy.

## Concepts covered

- [Asset Allocation](../concepts/asset-allocation.md): Portfolio composition and position limits express exposure choices and concentration constraints.
- [Investor Roles](../concepts/investor-roles.md): An investing approach should fit the time, expertise, and responsibility the investor actually assumes.
- [Opportunistic Cash](../concepts/opportunistic-cash.md): Accessible reserves allow an investor to fund opportunities without waiting to free capital.
- [Pairs Trading](../concepts/pairs-trading.md): A relative thesis compares two related investments rather than evaluating one in isolation.
- [Portfolio Review Cadence](../concepts/portfolio-review-cadence.md): Scheduled checks provide recurring occasions to inspect positions and make deliberate portfolio decisions.
- [Tax Efficiency](../concepts/tax-efficiency.md): Assess how account placement, timing, and applicable rules affect after-tax investment outcomes.
- [Tax Loss Harvesting](../concepts/tax-loss-harvesting.md): Realizing an investment loss may change tax outcomes, subject to applicable rules and timing.
