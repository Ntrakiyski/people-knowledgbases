# How to Speak Like the Top 0.01% (Copy This)

Sharran presents eight communication techniques. He begins with a why-what-how-now structure and three opening questions that establish relevance, urgency, and speaker credibility. He then distinguishes insight-story-lesson for memorable teaching from situation-conflict-resolution for explaining complex events. His client-dinner and book-launch accounts illustrate these frameworks; the claimed business outcomes and criticisms of bestseller systems remain his account.

For tense negotiations, he recommends agree-learned-helpful language to lower defensiveness. For leadership, he prefers sharing the diagnostic framework behind an answer so teams can develop judgment. He closes by explaining how a specific future vision can mobilize others and how a personal collection of useful phrases, rewritten in one’s own vocabulary, improves clarity over time.

## Concepts covered

- [Borrowed language](../concepts/borrowed-language.md): Capturing effective expressions, translating them into one’s own words, and saving them for reuse.
- [Communication Frameworks](../concepts/communication-frameworks.md): Structure explanations and difficult conversations so other people can understand and reuse them.
- [Storytelling Frameworks](../concepts/storytelling-frameworks.md): Give an insight meaning through a story, its tension, and a useful lesson.
