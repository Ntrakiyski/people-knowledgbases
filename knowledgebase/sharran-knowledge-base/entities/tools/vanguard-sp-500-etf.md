# Vanguard S&P 500 ETF (VOO)

> The first one is VOO.

[Once I Understood This About Money, My Life Changed](../../sources/once-i-understood-this-about-money-my-life-changed-XoWE1QPMFek.md), 0:15:15.

VOO appears in Sharran’s examples of giving his children exposure to large US businesses through small public-market holdings. He uses the fund to make equity ownership available before an investor can acquire a private company outright.

The lesson concerns the ownership habit: incoming money can buy an interest in productive assets rather than immediately become spending. The example also distinguishes owning businesses from personally operating one. The videos describe the speaker’s family practice and use simplified compounding scenarios; they do not establish a guaranteed return or a complete allocation plan. References using the ticker and the full fund name are combined on this page.
