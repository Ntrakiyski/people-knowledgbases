# Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities (TIPS)

> TIPS, which is Treasury inflation protected securities.

[31 Years of Wealth Knowledge in 18 Minutes](../../sources/31-years-of-wealth-knowledge-in-18-minutes-1VyRTwodPhY.md), 0:16:30.

Sharran discusses Treasury Inflation-Protected Securities as a way to keep purchasing power in view when holding money. He describes a connection to CPI and includes the securities in his broader discussion of cash management and inflation.

The explanation is simplified and sometimes describes the adjustment as an interest-rate feature. It does not comprehensively cover principal adjustments, taxes, purchase price, or returns over a particular holding period. The source therefore supports the speaker’s use of TIPS as an inflation-protection example, without establishing guaranteed after-tax purchasing power. TIP, the fund also discussed in the videos, has its own entity page because owning a traded fund and holding an individual Treasury security are distinct implementations.
