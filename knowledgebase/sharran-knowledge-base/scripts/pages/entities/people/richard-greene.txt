Sharran attributes a communication lesson to a coaching session with Richard Greene, whom he identifies as Tony Robbins’s speaking coach. The exercise exposed how many separate ideas Sharran was trying to communicate at once.

The resulting method packages an explanation into a small number of memorable parts, sometimes represented by a simple geometric figure. The point is to make the structure available to the listener, so that the explanation can be remembered and repeated. This is an attributed coaching anecdote, rather than a biography or an independent assessment of the coach. The transcript spells the surname “Green”; the quotations preserve the original text.
