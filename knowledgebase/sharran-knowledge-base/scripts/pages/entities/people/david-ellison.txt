Sharran presents David Ellison as contributing equity and obtaining family backing in the Paramount bidding narrative. His role connects owner commitment with lender confidence and the control associated with a transaction’s capital stack.

The conceptual lesson is that who supplies capital, on what terms, and with which assurances can matter as much as the quoted purchase price. Details of the bid, ownership, and financing are Sharran’s account rather than independently verified transaction reporting.
