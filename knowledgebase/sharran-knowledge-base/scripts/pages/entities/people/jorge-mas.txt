Sharran presents Jorge Mas as the dealmaker preparing Inter Miami for Messi well before the final agreement. His account emphasizes ownership arrangements, family logistics, and the alignment of commercial partners. Preparation expands what can be offered when the opportunity becomes available.

The lesson is broader than negotiating a larger salary: understanding another party’s practical priorities can make otherwise difficult terms workable. Mas is used as the example of someone assembling those conditions in advance. The details are Sharran’s reconstruction of the transaction, rather than verified contract history or evidence that every described step caused the eventual agreement.
