Sharran uses Berkshire Hathaway to illustrate patient ownership combined with available capital. His examples describe retaining substantial cash and acting when market conditions create an attractive opportunity. Cash has a purpose in this framework: it lets an investor act without first selling another holding.

The example complements the channel’s emphasis on long holding periods and investment selectivity. It does not establish a single cash percentage suitable for every portfolio. Sharran’s references to Berkshire’s holdings, homebuilder investments, historical balances, and returns are his narrated examples; the knowledge base does not treat them as a current holdings report.
