Sharran uses The Real Brokerage to illustrate a clear value proposition and a repeatable customer-acquisition process. He describes several potential income streams for agents and explains how speaking engagements, Instagram promotion, and follow-up conversations can connect attention with a business opportunity.

The company’s role is to make the sales funnel concrete: identify an audience, state an understandable benefit, and create a route into conversation. Reported agent counts, valuation changes, and income opportunities are Sharran’s narrated claims. The source does not establish that every participant receives the same result or that a described funnel alone caused the company’s growth.
