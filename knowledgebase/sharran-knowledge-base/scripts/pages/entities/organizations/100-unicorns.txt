Sharran uses 100 Unicorns as an example of giving a child responsibility inside a real business. In his account, he and another father acquired the site and involved his daughter in orders and customer interactions. The educational value came from having actual people depend on her work.

The example belongs to his broader approach to financial education: let children experience value creation before treating money as an abstract balance. He says substantial profit was not the main objective. The transcript therefore supports a lesson about practical responsibility, rather than a claim that acquiring a small online business is a dependable source of family income.
