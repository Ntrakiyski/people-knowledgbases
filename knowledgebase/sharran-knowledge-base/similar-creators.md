# Five creators similar to Sharran Srivatsaa

**Start with Codie Sanchez for ownership and acquisitions, then Alex Hormozi for business frameworks.** Dan Martell, Nick Huber, and Sahil Bloom complement different parts of Sharran's teaching.

This is an editorial ranking based on the [Sharran creator profile](creator-profile.md), researched on **2026-11-16**. It prioritizes ownership and wealth mechanics, then practical business execution, frameworks, and freedom. It is a selected shortlist, not an exhaustive ranking of every creator or a ranking by followers.

## 1. Codie Sanchez — closest for ownership and acquisitions

**[YouTube: @CodieSanchezCT](https://www.youtube.com/@CodieSanchezCT) · [X: @Codie_Sanchez](https://x.com/Codie_Sanchez)**

Codie's official material centers on buying and scaling cash-flowing small businesses, including overlooked businesses outside fashionable startup categories. Her ownership-first approach closely matches Sharran's move from earned income to assets, and his interest in acquisition financing and deal structure.

**Best fit:** You want to learn about buying an existing business, financing a purchase, and improving its operations.

**Difference:** Her positioning is more concentrated on small-business acquisitions. Sharran's imported catalog ranges further into portfolio construction, communication, and family wealth.

**Start with:** the business-buying and business-scaling material at [Contrarian Thinking](https://www.contrarianthinking.co/), then use her YouTube channel to explore those subjects. This is a topic suggestion, not a claim to have evaluated every course or video.

**Evidence:** [Codie's official site](https://codiesanchez.com/) describes her business-ownership mission and links both accounts. [Contrarian Thinking](https://www.contrarianthinking.co/) explicitly focuses on buying and scaling cash-flowing businesses.

## 2. Alex Hormozi — closest for practical business frameworks

**[YouTube: @AlexHormozi](https://www.youtube.com/@AlexHormozi) · [X: @AlexHormozi](https://x.com/AlexHormozi)**

Alex's official material focuses on offers, customer acquisition, monetization, and scaling. His use of reusable business models makes him a strong match for Sharran's emphasis on value propositions, execution, focus, and teaching a decision process.

**Best fit:** You already have a business or skill and want to improve how it attracts customers and makes money.

**Difference:** The reviewed material emphasizes building business revenue and profit more than Sharran's family-wealth and investment-allocation teaching.

**Start with:** the free material associated with **$100M Offers**, **$100M Leads**, and **$100M Money Models**, linked from his official page.

**Relationship to Sharran:** This recommendation comes from the same Acquisition.com network. Sharran also describes working with the Hormozis in the [imported corpus](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BZrgkgCF79g&t=804s). That explains some similarity; it is not independent validation of either creator's claims.

**Evidence:** [Alex's official Acquisition.com page](https://www.acquisition.com/about-alex-hormozi-acquisition) describes those topics, links his YouTube channel, and embeds posts from his X account.

## 3. Dan Martell — closest for systems and time freedom

**[YouTube: @danmartell](https://www.youtube.com/@danmartell) · [X: @danmartell](https://x.com/danmartell)**

Dan's official positioning emphasizes scaling a business that runs without the founder and reclaiming time. His **Buy Back Your Time** material connects business systems with the freedom to choose how to live, matching Sharran's operating-framework and optionality themes.

**Best fit:** Your business depends too heavily on your personal involvement and you want to improve delegation and systems.

**Difference:** His reviewed material leans toward founders, CEOs, and software businesses. Sharran spends more of this corpus on personal investing and transaction mechanics.

**Start with:** **Buy Back Your Time** and his material on building a business that runs without you.

**Evidence:** [Dan's official site](https://www.danmartell.com/) describes these themes and links both accounts. Its business-results testimonials were not used as evidence for this ranking.

## 4. Nick Huber — closest for operating businesses and real estate

**[YouTube: Nick Huber – Sweaty Startup](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCBYwRdRw6x9bH4PsyCLnmOw) · [X: @sweatystartup](https://x.com/sweatystartup)**

Nick's official site combines entrepreneurship, real estate, hiring, delegation, and long-term wealth. This aligns with Sharran's interest in productive assets and the operating work behind ownership.

**Best fit:** You want business and property ownership discussed alongside hiring and day-to-day execution.

**Difference:** His reviewed positioning concentrates on entrepreneurship and real estate. It offers less overlap with Sharran's communication frameworks and family-wealth structures.

**Start with:** **The Sweaty Startup Podcast** for operating businesses and **The Nick Huber Show** for real estate, entrepreneurship, and wealth; both are linked from his site.

**Evidence:** [The official Sweaty Startup site](https://www.sweatystartup.com/) describes these shows, lists hiring and delegation material, and links his YouTube and X accounts.

## 5. Sahil Bloom — closest for wealth frameworks and life design

**[YouTube: Sahil Bloom](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC0vZSXQHRGPNZMFAKPLJcww) · [X: @SahilBloom](https://x.com/SahilBloom)**

Sahil's official material combines finance, business, actionable ideas, and building a healthy, wealthy life. **The 5 Types of Wealth** makes him a useful match for the freedom and life-design side of Sharran's profile. His site also describes a holding company of cash-flowing businesses.

**Best fit:** You want to connect wealth building with broader choices about time, health, relationships, and how to live.

**Difference:** He is the broadest match here. The reviewed material supports a stronger fit for life and wealth frameworks than for acquisition financing or detailed deal analysis.

**Start with:** **The 5 Types of Wealth** and **The Curiosity Chronicle**, linked from his official site.

**Evidence:** [Sahil's official site](https://www.sahilbloom.com/) describes those subjects and links his YouTube channel and legacy Twitter account, `twitter.com/SahilBloom`, presented above with the equivalent X domain.
