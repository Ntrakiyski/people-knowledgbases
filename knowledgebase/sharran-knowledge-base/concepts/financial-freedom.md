# Financial Freedom

> This is where your passive income is greater than your monthly expenses.

[Once I Hit These Money Milestones, I Became Financially Free](../sources/once-i-hit-these-money-milestones-i-became-financially-free-aetr9LovwE4.md), 0:12:54.

Sharran defines financial freedom as a relationship between recurring passive income and monthly expenses. A prestigious salary, a particular age, or a large net-worth number does not by itself establish that relationship. The practical question is whether ordinary life still requires the next unit of active work.

His example pairs $12,000 of monthly passive income with $10,000 of expenses. The point is the coverage margin, not either dollar amount as a universal target. Someone with higher recurring costs needs more income to reach the same condition. This makes control of lifestyle growth part of the freedom calculation rather than a separate budgeting exercise.

The money used to generate those payments must first be accumulated or the relevant effort performed. His milestone sequences therefore begin earlier: active income covers spending, surplus is routed deliberately, a reserve protects against interruption, and assets are built. Different videos package these steps differently, but their endpoint is consistent.

He also distinguishes the crossing point from later abundance. One framework continues from income coverage to owning more assets and working mainly for enjoyment. Freedom is thus an important transition within his system, not a claim that no financial uncertainty remains afterward.

This is why he describes the hardest change as moving from work funding the whole lifestyle to previously built assets or effort doing more of that job. Waiting for one large business sale delays construction of that income system; small ongoing allocations begin it earlier.

The desired result is choice over the day: continuing to work because the activity is meaningful, rather than because each month's expenses force it. His intermediate dollar thresholds are proposed waypoints, while expense coverage is the definition he repeats across sources.
