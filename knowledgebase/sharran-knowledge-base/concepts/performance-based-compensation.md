# Performance-based compensation

> be willing to trade skills for performance-based [music] compensation

[Lionel Messi’s $1.5B Deal Can Make You Rich (Here’s How)](../sources/lionel-messi-s-1-5b-deal-can-make-you-rich-here-s-how-FBZxj9CGk00.md), 0:13:23.

Srivatsaa describes performance based compensation as participating in economic value one helps create. A fixed salary pays an agreed amount for a role; a bonus, revenue share, or ownership interest can make part of the outcome depend on the commercial result. His central example is his analysis of Lionel Messi’s move to Inter Miami.

## Separate the layers of compensation

In that account, base salary is only one part of the comparison. Srivatsaa discusses potential participation in subscription demand, merchandise activity, and future club ownership. His reported deal amounts, percentages, and contract details remain claims in the video rather than independently verified terms.

The mechanism is a chain of created value: the player’s performance and brand attract attention, attention raises interest in the club and league, and that interest may support subscriptions and merchandise sales. Contingent compensation attempts to connect the contributor’s reward to those broader effects.

## More upside means accepting a different dependency

Srivatsaa translates the example into three instructions for professionals: develop valuable earning skills, consider exchanging some guaranteed pay for greater performance upside, and recognize when ownership might capture longer term value. The order matters in his account. The proposed trade relies on a capability that other parties value, rather than merely a willingness to receive less cash.

He also acknowledges that several parts of the Messi commercial arrangement had to work together. That is the distinction between an advertised opportunity and money already earned. A lower salary cannot be compared with potential ownership or bonuses as though all payments had equal certainty or timing.

His motivational conclusion that creating value can almost guarantee wealth exceeds what the example proves. The grounded teaching is about compensation structure: identify the result being rewarded, the period over which it is earned, and the execution on which the additional participation depends.
