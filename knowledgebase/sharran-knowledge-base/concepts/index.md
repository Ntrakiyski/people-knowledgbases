# Concepts

Ideas, techniques, patterns, and mental models mined from the videos. Grouped by theme.

## Wealth foundations

- [Income Margin](income-margin.md) - The surplus between earnings and spending that funds future assets and financial resilience.
- [Transition Fund](transition-fund.md) - A reserve covering months of expenses buys time to replace or reinvent active income.
- [Passive Income](passive-income.md) - Income prefunded with money or effort can reduce dependence on ongoing paid work.
- [Financial Freedom](financial-freedom.md) - Financial freedom means recurring passive income covers spending and makes work optional.
- [Debt Management](debt-management.md) - A clear repayment plan combines affordability, incentives, and sustained execution.
- [Tiny Income Generating Assets](tiny-income-generating-assets.md) - Small productive assets offer an initial practical step toward recurring investment income.
- [Wealth Ladder](wealth-ladder.md) - A sequence connecting earned income, controlled spending, owned assets, and recurring investment income.
- [Invest First](invest-first.md) - Convert a deliberate share of income into assets before expanding lifestyle spending.
- [Automated Money Routing](automated-money-routing.md) - Distribute incoming money explicitly among lifestyle spending, taxes, and future assets.
- [Financial education for children](financial-education-for-children.md) - Teaching value creation, investing, and stewardship through real economic participation.
- [Donor-advised giving](donor-advised-giving.md) - A dedicated giving account used to separate contribution timing from later charity selection.
- [Dynasty trust](dynasty-trust.md) - A multigenerational asset-holding structure designed to preserve family capital and distribute benefits.
- [Family Governance](family-governance.md) - Coordinate family ownership, control, distributions, and transfers through rules established before conflict.

## Investing and risk

- [Compound Growth](compound-growth.md) - Returns can earn further returns as an invested balance grows over time.
- [Accredited Investing](accredited-investing.md) - Investor eligibility can expand access to private market offerings.
- [Conviction and Investment Rationale](conviction-investing.md) - Know what you own, why you own it, and which evidence would change the decision.
- [Opportunistic Cash](opportunistic-cash.md) - Accessible reserves allow an investor to fund opportunities without waiting to free capital.
- [Investment X-Ray](investment-x-ray.md) - Evaluate capital preservation, tax efficiency, cash flow, and growth before committing money.
- [Equity Ownership](equity-ownership.md) - Participate in business value through ownership, with the corresponding capital and execution risks.
- [Risk Management](risk-management.md) - Evaluate downside, obligations, behavioral errors, and execution dependence before and after committing resources.
- [Long Term Investing](long-term-investing.md) - Patient asset ownership can create a different wealth path from repeatedly earning transaction profits.
- [Bonds and Debt Capital](bonds.md) - Debt claims, issuer types, and repayment risks across public bonds and private lending.
- [Cash Management](cash-management.md) - Match liquid reserves and surplus holdings to access needs, returns, and the risks of each instrument.
- [Asset Allocation](asset-allocation.md) - Portfolio composition and position limits express exposure choices and concentration constraints.
- [Pairs Trading](pairs-trading.md) - A relative thesis compares two related investments rather than evaluating one in isolation.
- [Investor Roles](investor-roles.md) - An investing approach should fit the time, expertise, and responsibility the investor actually assumes.
- [Portfolio Review Cadence](portfolio-review-cadence.md) - Scheduled checks provide recurring occasions to inspect positions and make deliberate portfolio decisions.
- [Financial Literacy and Investment Incentives](investment-incentives.md) - Understand investment products, costs, and how the people recommending them are paid.
- [Investment fee drag](investment-fee-drag.md) - Recurring investment fees reduce both current returns and the future growth of capital.
- [Cash-flow investing](cash-flow-investing.md) - Assess whether an investment pays income while it is held, separately from appreciation.
- [Inflation protection](inflation-protection.md) - Evaluate investment results in purchasing power as well as nominal dollars.
- [Asset-backed borrowing](asset-backed-borrowing.md) - Using valuable holdings as collateral to access liquidity without selling them.
- [Financial leverage](financial-leverage.md) - Using borrowed capital or collateral to magnify exposure relative to the investor’s own equity.

- [Acquisition Financing](acquisition-financing.md) - Combine payment terms, obligations, and capital sources to fund a business purchase.
- [Value Proposition](value-proposition.md) - A concise promise explains why a specific customer chooses the business.
- [Customer Discovery](customer-discovery.md) - Direct conversations reveal the benefits customers actually value rather than the seller’s assumptions.
- [Customer Acquisition Cost](customer-acquisition-cost.md) - Acquisition cost measures the resources spent to bring in a customer and affects growth sustainability.
- [Sales Funnels](sales-funnels.md) - A repeatable sequence turns a defined audience’s attention into conversations and customer decisions.
- [Deal structure](deal-structure.md) - A transaction’s terms distribute control, ownership, obligations, and future upside.
- [Capital stack](capital-stack.md) - The capital stack maps the financing and ownership contributions supporting a deal.
- [Intellectual property ownership](intellectual-property-ownership.md) - Reusable creative rights can participate in many future revenue streams.
- [Sweat equity](sweat-equity.md) - Ownership can be earned by contributing work and capability rather than only cash.
- [Strategic optionality](strategic-optionality.md) - Build choices into a business rather than depending on a single exit outcome.
- [Competitive moat](competitive-moat.md) - Difficult upfront work can build an advantage that supports future monetization.
- [Acquihire](acquihire.md) - A deal can prioritize acquiring a team’s capabilities and attention over buying its product.
- [Negotiation Frameworks](negotiation-frameworks.md) - Use objections to revise terms, reduce execution uncertainty, and understand what could enable agreement.
- [Home Buying Game Plan](home-buying-game-plan.md) - A structured process for defining housing goals, arranging financing, searching, and negotiating.
- [Performance-based compensation](performance-based-compensation.md) - Compensation linked to measurable commercial results rather than only fixed salary.

## Execution and judgment

- [Constraint Driven Problem Solving](constraint-driven-problem-solving.md) - A deliberate limit can reveal solutions obscured by the obvious use of available resources.
- [Skill Development](skill-development.md) - Acquire and combine useful capabilities through focused learning, practice, and real application.
- [Intentional Resource Allocation](intentional-resource-allocation.md) - Investing means deliberately deploying available resources toward a chosen outcome.
- [Time Management](time-management.md) - Make finite time visible and assign bounded attention to deliberately selected activities.
- [Career fit](career-fit.md) - Choose work that matches personal fit rather than professional status alone.
- [Decision Frameworks](decision-framework.md) - Define the context, evidence, deadline, and exit conditions for consequential choices.
- [Focus and Sustained Effort](focus.md) - Concentrate repeated effort on a small number of useful activities and make improvement observable.
- [Persistence](persistence.md) - Keep pursuing meaningful work using a practical rule for difficult days.
- [Input–output equation](input-output-equation.md) - Identify and execute the repeatable inputs behind a desired result.
- [Speed formula](speed-formula.md) - Move from opportunity to plan to action while minimizing unnecessary delay.
- [Graded exposure](graded-exposure.md) - Building tolerance for judgment by moving from low-stakes practice to broader exposure.
- [After-action review](after-action-review.md) - Comparing intended and actual results to identify a gap and change the next attempt.
- [Default yes](default-yes.md) - Approaching opportunities by asking how they could work instead of automatically finding reasons to reject them.
- [High-performance environment](high-performance-environment.md) - A peer setting that stretches effort while preserving a sense of belonging and capability.
