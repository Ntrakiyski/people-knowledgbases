# Equity Ownership

> Because both of these vehicles generate equity.

[It Took Me 31 Years to Learn This About Getting Rich](../sources/it-took-me-31-years-to-learn-this-about-getting-rich-o3L2xWA4hF0.md), 0:04:27.

Sharran distinguishes money received for current work from participation in an asset’s future value. Cash flow pays today’s bills; equity gives the owner exposure to what a company or property may become. His recurring instruction is to convert some present earnings into ownership rather than rely indefinitely on personal labor alone.

He calls businesses and real estate the golden stairways because they can combine operating income with an asset that has a sale value. A business’s earnings and the multiple a buyer applies can make its ownership value substantially different from one year’s pay. For people unable or unwilling to acquire a private company or building, he discusses publicly traded business and real-estate funds as accessible forms of ownership exposure.

The distinction also changes compensation negotiations. In his account of Messi’s Inter Miami arrangement, salary is only one layer alongside revenue participation and prospective equity. Accepting less guaranteed cash can create participation in value a contributor helps build, but the result depends on the enterprise and its execution. The reported contract figures are Sharran’s account, rather than independently confirmed economics.

His own move from adviser to entrepreneur supplies the corresponding cost. A client’s sale proceeds made advisory fees seem small by comparison, yet Sharran also describes committing personal capital, financing his stake, entering an unfamiliar industry, and working without compensation for an extended period. An experienced chairman helped him learn but did not remove the operating risk.

Ownership is consequently a different economic position, not a promise that entrepreneurship always pays more. It brings exposure to failure and potentially illiquid value as well as growth. Current cash needs and the ability to bear that uncertainty remain distinct from the appeal of a future sale.
