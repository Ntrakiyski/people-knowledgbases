# Default yes

> It forces your brain instead of asking can I do this, it switches it to how can I do this?

[Being Around The Top 0.01% Teaches You This](../sources/being-around-the-top-0-01-teaches-you-this-BZrgkgCF79g.md), 0:07:46.

Srivatsaa’s default yes is a way of approaching an opportunity before reflexively rejecting it. The intended shift is from asking whether one can do something to asking how it could be made to work. It is a posture for generating possibilities, rather than a complete evaluation of every commitment.

He attributes the phrase to Four Seasons founder Isadore Sharp during hospitality training. In his account, Sharp repeatedly expressed that the answer was yes and the next task was to understand the question. Srivatsaa extends that service principle to experiments, challenges, and unexpected opportunities in his own life.

## Make implementation a question worth examining

An automatic no can end thought before the constraints are understood. A provisional yes directs attention toward resources, an approach, or a next action. Srivatsaa argues that this produces momentum and greater exposure to luck because more possibilities receive an attempt instead of an immediate dismissal.

His practical suggestion is a one week experiment in treating yes as the starting answer. The evidence supports describing that experiment and his rationale; it does not demonstrate that accepting every request improves outcomes or that optimism removes tradeoffs.

## A starting posture is different from an irreversible decision

This lesson appears alongside his separate emphasis on reversibility and thoughtful judgment. An opportunity that can be tried and undone has different consequences from one that puts important resources permanently at risk. Default yes therefore needs to remain attached to its role in the teaching: opening an implementation question rather than declaring every financial or business proposal worthy of commitment.

The mechanism is simple but specific. Notice the instinctive rejection, ask what would make a yes possible, and examine the resulting route. The value in Srivatsaa’s account comes from discovering options that habitual skepticism would never have allowed into consideration.
